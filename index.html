亞馬遜跟隨微軟放棄數據中心保密協議，AI 算力基礎設施的社區信任成本正在上升Amazon follows Microsoft in dropping data center NDAs as community opposition to AI infrastructure grows

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • 亞馬遜宣布在數據中心選址談判中放棄保密協議，微軟此前已作出與樣承諾，標誌著行業透明化壓力上升。Amazon will waive NDAs in data center siting negotiations, following Microsoft's earlier commitment, as industry transparency pressure rises.
  • 紐約與舊金山等城市已相繼通過針對 AI 數據中心的建設暫停令，迫使業界以主動溝通換取建設許可。New York and San Francisco have passed data center moratoriums, forcing AI companies to trade community disclosure for building permits.
  • 分析師指出，單靠公開資訊能否化解社區反彈仍是未知數，算力供給的中長期穩定性存在相應風險。Analysts question whether transparency alone will resolve deep-seated resistance, with long-term compute supply stability remaining uncertain.

亞馬遜於2026年10月3日在官方部落格AboutAmazon.com發表一篇題為「共同建設數據中心」的文章，正式宣佈放棄在與地方政府談判數據中心選址時慣用的保密協議（NDA）。此舉緊隨微軟早先透過「Local Microsoft」部落格的類似公告，標誌著兩大雲端巨頭在公共關係策略上的明顯轉向——將「社區優先」作為獲得建設許可的新敘事框架。Amazon published a post on AboutAmazon.com on October 3, 2026, announcing it would no longer require non-disclosure agreements when negotiating data center locations with local governments. The announcement was framed under a "building together with communities" narrative and followed a similar pledge from Microsoft via its "Local Microsoft" blog earlier in 2026, signaling a coordinated industry shift toward community-first public relations.

這一轉變的直接背景是美國多地政府對大型數據中心的強力管制。紐約州已通過暫停令，凍結新建大型數據中心的審批，期限延續至州環境審查程序完成，預計約需一年；三藩市則在近期正式批准了數據中心禁令，被與會分析師形容為「相當諷刺」——這座以科技聞名的城市，卻成為AI基礎設施建設最明顯的障礙之一。值得注意的是，紐約暫停令的措辭保留彈性空間，實質上是「研究期」，而非絕對禁止，地方政府仍可視情況逐案審批。The backdrop is a wave of municipal resistance. New York State passed a moratorium freezing permits for large new data centers, pending completion of an environmental review process expected to take roughly one year — though the order's language preserves wiggle room for case-by-case approvals. San Francisco recently approved its own data center ban, described by TechCrunch panelists as "pretty ironic" given the city's tech identity. Both measures reflect growing municipal-level demand for transparency before approvals are granted.

保密協議原本是科技企業與地方政府談判的慣用工具。據TechCrunch記者Sean O'Kane分析，NDA的存在讓企業得以在公眾知曉前向政府提出更大範圍的利益交換要求，例如稅收優惠或配套基礎設施承諾。他以Uber於2010年代中期在奧蘭多附近與地方政府秘密達成的稅務補貼協議作為歷史參照，指出此類秘密談判模式一旦曝光，往往引發嚴重的公眾信任危機。分析師Rebecca Bellan則對暫停令能否真正延遲數據中心落地持懷疑態度：暫停令通常持續一至兩年，而一座數據中心從獲批到上線往往需要兩至五年，兩個時間軸並不重疊，因此政治阻力未必能轉化為實質性的建設延誤。NDAs had historically served as a negotiating tool allowing companies to seek broader concessions — tax incentives, infrastructure commitments — before details became public. TechCrunch's Sean O'Kane cited Uber's secretive tax subsidy deal near Orlando in the mid-2010s as a cautionary parallel: once such arrangements surfaced, public trust collapsed. Fellow analyst Rebecca Bellan questioned whether moratoriums will meaningfully delay buildouts, noting they typically last one to two years while data centers take two to five-plus years from approval to operation — the timelines don't overlap. Analyst Anthony Ha described dropping NDAs as "a good first step" while noting it is unlikely to immediately convert opponents, and observed that the industry has shifted away from "this is inevitable" messaging toward genuine justification.

然而，分析師普遍認為放棄NDA只是「良好的第一步」，難以即時化解根本反對情緒。行業的溝通問題積累已久——O'Kane直言「業界在解釋自身時做得極差」，企業長期採取「這是大勢所趨，加入吧」的姿態，如今已開始轉向主動提供正當性論述，這本身就是一種信號，說明社區阻力已具備足夠的政治份量，迫使企業改變策略。對香港與台灣的行銷從業者而言，這一趨勢提示未來AI算力服務的採購與定價，將愈來愈受到數據中心選址成本、合規要求與社區關係成本的影響；企業在評估AI雲端服務供應商的長期穩定性時，地緣政治與在地社區風險應納入考量。For marketers in Hong Kong and Taiwan, the implication is structural: as social license costs rise for AI infrastructure in Western markets, cloud pricing, SLA reliability, and long-term capacity commitments from major providers may be affected. Evaluating AI vendor stability increasingly requires accounting for geopolitical and community-relations risk alongside traditional technical criteria.

PolyAI 與 Otter 高管：語音 AI 尚未達到「ChatGPT 時刻」，多語混合環境下的 ASR 準確率是落地主要障礙Voice AI executives say the technology has yet to hit its 'ChatGPT moment' — ASR failures in mixed-language markets remain the core barrier

  • PolyAI 技術長指出，ASR 在粵語、普通話與英語混合環境下的漏判率，導致下游全流程出錯，是語音 AI 商業落地的最大障礙。PolyAI's CTO says ASR transcription failures in mixed Cantonese, Mandarin, and English environments cause cascading downstream errors, blocking commercial voice AI scale.
  • Otter 行銷長強調，AI 語音代理必須主動告知用戶其非人類身份，以符合消費者信任與合規要求。Otter's CMO stresses that AI voice agents must proactively disclose their non-human identity to meet consumer trust and compliance standards.

PolyAI技術長Shawn Wen與Otter行銷長Alex Gay於2026年10月接受TechCrunch記者Ivan Mehta採訪，兩人同時指出語音AI目前仍未出現如ChatGPT於2022年11月推出後所引發的消費者突破時刻。儘管投資者已向語音AI新創企業注入數十億美元，模型廠商、企業客服、會議工具、AI語音輸入等細分賽道均吸引到充裕資金，宣稱具備接近人類對話能力的新工具每週都有發佈，而市場行銷訴求與真實應用效果之間的落差，卻始終未能彌合。PolyAI CTO Shawn Wen and Otter CMO Alex Gay told TechCrunch reporter Ivan Mehta in October 2026 that voice AI has yet to reach an inflection point comparable to ChatGPT's mainstream breakthrough in November 2022. Despite billions of dollars flowing into voice AI startups across model makers, enterprise customer service, meeting tools, and AI dictation, and despite new tools claiming near-human conversational ability launching weekly, the gap between marketing claims and real-world performance has not closed.

兩位高管各自點出不同技術瓶頸。Gay從會議轉錄的角度說明，自動語音識別（ASR）系統若在轉錄中遺漏關鍵詞語，將導致整個下游處理管線失效——摘要錯誤、自動化行動觸發錯誤，最終使用者因系統採取了錯誤行動而喪失信任，「一旦它開始執行錯誤的動作，你就會失去對平台的信任」。他特別強調語言能力的改善是當前最迫切的課題，多語混合環境（如粵語夾英語、普通話夾術語）對現有ASR系統的準確率構成系統性挑戰，而非個案異常。Wen則從模型推理速度切入：目前全雙工語音模型（即可同時說話與收聽）在技術上已可實現，而推理速度仍不足以支撐自然對話的節奏，「下一個挑戰是讓推理變得足夠快，使模型能夠迅速提取答案」。The two executives identified distinct technical bottlenecks. Gay, speaking from the perspective of meeting transcription, explained that ASR errors on key words corrupt the entire downstream pipeline — summaries go wrong, automated actions misfire, and once the system takes the wrong action, users lose trust in the platform entirely. He singled out language accuracy in mixed-language environments as the most urgent gap: code-switching scenarios (such as Cantonese-English or Mandarin with technical jargon) challenge current ASR systems systematically, not as edge cases. Wen focused on reasoning latency: full-duplex voice models — capable of speaking and listening simultaneously — now exist technically, but reasoning speed remains too slow to sustain natural conversational pacing. "The next challenge is to make reasoning very fast, so that the models can fetch answers quickly," he said.

在監管與信任架構方面，兩家公司採取了不同層次的披露策略。PolyAI要求所有企業客戶在通話中向終端使用者明確告知對方正在與AI通話，這是其產品部署的強制前提；Otter的做法則更進一步，即使其AI機器人未出席會議，只要有錄製行為發生，也會主動透過聊天功能通知所有與會者。兩人均認為，主動披露是建立使用者信任的必要條件，而非法規壓力下的被動合規舉措。Wen另外提及，當語音品質達到足夠水準後，使用者會開始建立信心，形成正向循環，而這一臨界點目前仍未穩定觸及。On disclosure, both companies have adopted proactive stances. PolyAI mandates that enterprise clients inform end users they are speaking with an AI before deploying its product. Otter goes further: even when its AI bot is absent from a meeting, it notifies all participants via chat that recording is taking place. Both executives framed disclosure not as regulatory compliance but as a prerequisite for building genuine user trust. Ott is also developing a "digital twin" feature — AI avatars representing real individuals in meetings with human-like emotive expression — and Gay argued that without emotional authenticity, "it's just a Q&A chatbot."

Otter正在開發「數位分身」功能，讓AI化身在會議中代表真實人物出席，並展現具情感表達力的人類化表情，Gay直言若這一層面做不到位，「那只是一個問答聊天機器人而已」。對香港與台灣的行銷團隊而言，現階段部署語音AI於客服或電話行銷需正視粵語、普通話與英語混用的ASR準確率限制；在簡報、提案或內容生產流程中引入語音轉文字工具前，應先在本地語言環境進行系統性驗收測試，而非依賴廠商的通用基準數據。For marketing teams in Hong Kong and Taiwan, the near-term practical takeaway is to test ASR accuracy against local mixed-language patterns before deploying voice AI in customer service or telemarketing; vendor benchmark figures based on monolingual corpora are unlikely to reflect real-world performance in bilingual or code-switching environments.

OpenAI 正式確認，旗下最早期的 GPT-4o API 快照版本 gpt-4o-2024-05-13 將於 2026 年 10 月 23 日終止服務，此日期距離公告發出僅餘約 11 天。這一版本以其首次公開發布日期命名——2024 年 5 月 13 日，正是 OpenAI 執行長 Sam Altman 在社群媒體上以一個字「her」宣告 GPT-4o 問世的那一天，刻意致敬史蒂芬·史匹柏的科幻電影《雲端情人》。然而近兩年後，這個版本的 API 存取將正式畫下句點，仍依賴此快照運行的自動化工作流程若未完成遷移，將面臨服務中斷。OpenAI has confirmed that its earliest GPT-4o API snapshot — gpt-4o-2024-05-13 — will be retired on October 23, 2026, leaving fewer than 11 days from the announcement for affected users to migrate. The version is named after its public debut date, May 13, 2024, when CEO Sam Altman posted a single word on social media — 'her' — in a deliberate nod to the film Her, signaling a new era of expressive AI conversation. Automated workflows still hardcoded to this snapshot will break on retirement day if not updated in time.

OpenAI 援引使用數據為理據：截至退役日，選擇 gpt-4o-2024-05-13 的日活用戶比例僅餘 0.1%，大部分用戶已轉移至 GPT-5.2。然而反對者指出，這個數字並不能真實反映用戶意願——ChatGPT 介面預設自動導向最新模型，免費用戶更無法手動切換至舊版，使得「自願遷移」與「被迫遷移」難以區分。一份研究分析約 1,500 篇與「Keep4o」社群相關的貼文，論文標題直白道出用戶心聲：《請不要殺死唯一仍有人情味的模型》（Please don't kill the only model that still feels human）。這場圍繞 AI 模型「個性」的爭議，在商業 API 政策討論中實屬罕見。OpenAI cited usage metrics to justify the decision: only 0.1% of daily active users still selected gpt-4o-2024-05-13 by the time of retirement, with the majority having moved to GPT-5.2. Critics, however, challenge the validity of this figure — ChatGPT's interface defaults to the latest model automatically, and free-tier users have no option to manually select older versions, making it impossible to distinguish genuine preference from passive displacement. A study analyzing roughly 1,500 posts from the Keep4o community captured the sentiment in its title: 'Please don't kill the only model that still feels human.' This debate over AI model 'personality' is unusual in the context of commercial API policy.

反對退役的聲浪從 2025 年 4 月起就已成形。Change.org 上一份「請保留 GPT-4o」的聯署請願，截至報道發出時已累積 23,930 個簽名；另有一份要求以非商業研究授權公開模型權重的聯署，則僅獲 472 人支持，顯示大多數用戶關注的是服務延續，而非開源取得。2026 年 2 月，約 30 名支持者甚至在 OpenAI 舊金山總部外守夜，進行象徵性抗議。奧地利數位權利組織主導的 opensource4o 聯盟亦於同年 7 月發表聲明，要求 OpenAI 以 Apache 2.0 授權釋出 2025 年 3 月版本的模型權重，理由是 2024 年系統安全卡評估該模型在網路安全、生物風險與模型自主性三項指標上均為低風險等級，具備公開條件。這場圍繞舊版 AI 模型保存的運動，折射出用戶對 AI「個性一致性」的高度重視，以及對廠商單方面終止服務的廣泛不安。Organized opposition began as early as April 2025. A Change.org petition titled 'Please Keep GPT-4o Available' had gathered 23,930 signatures as of the article's publication date. A separate petition requesting non-commercial research access to the model weights drew only 472 signatures — indicating that most users prioritize service continuity over open-source access. In February 2026, approximately 30 supporters held a vigil outside OpenAI's San Francisco headquarters.
