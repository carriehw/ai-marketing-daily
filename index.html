Unlike previous conversational assistants, Duet is designed to operate continuously in the background, monitoring connected services, proactively assembling briefings and dashboards, and surfacing recommendations without waiting for a user prompt. This shift from passive tool to active workflow participant represents one of the most significant architectural changes in enterprise AI tooling since the initial wave of LLM integrations.

Claude Duet 以 Claude Opus 5.5 為底層推理引擎，整合超過 6,000 個第三方連接器，涵蓋 CRM、電郵、日曆、電商及財務管理等主流企業工具。其最受關注的功能是透過 Stripe Link 與 Shopify Pay 代理用戶完成實際付款——這在市場上屬首例，直接模糊了 AI 助理與 AI 代理執行者之間的界線。為確保安全，每筆交易均須用戶逐一明確審批，且系統內建敏感資料分類器，設計上保證信用卡號碼、身份證件等私人資訊不會外洩至外部服務。目前產品以「研究預覽」形式向 Pro 及 Max 訂閱用戶開放候補申請，支援 iOS、Android 及 Claude.ai 網頁版。Built on Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Duet connects to over 6,000 third-party services including CRM platforms, email clients, calendars, e-commerce tools, and financial systems. Its most technically novel feature is the ability to execute real payments on behalf of users through Stripe Link and Shopify Pay — the first agentic AI product to directly close the loop between analysis and financial action. Safety guardrails require explicit per-transaction user approval, and a built-in sensitive data classifier prevents leakage of credit card numbers or government ID details into downstream services. The product is currently in research preview for Pro and Max subscribers via waitlist, available on iOS, Android, and the Claude.ai web app.

Anthropic 於 2026 年 10 月宣布將 Agent Skills 開放標準，連同相關品牌、GitHub 組織及 agentskills.io 網域，正式移交 Linux Foundation 旗下的 Agentic AI Foundation（AAIF）。AAIF 由 Anthropic、Block 與 OpenAI 共同創辦，是目前主要 AI 廠商中罕見的橫向合作機制。這一舉措延續了 Anthropic 早前將 MCP（Model Context Protocol）捐贈予同一基金會的策略，形成「制定標準、推廣普及、捐贈治理」的完整路徑，目的在於防止任何單一企業對核心 AI 代理基礎設施形成壟斷控制。In October 2026, Anthropic transferred the Agent Skills open standard — along with its brand, GitHub organization, and the agentskills.io domain — to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation. AAIF is co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI, representing one of the few instances of cross-vendor horizontal cooperation among leading AI companies. This move continues a deliberate pattern established when Anthropic donated MCP to the same foundation: develop and popularize a standard, then transfer governance to a neutral body, preventing any single company from controlling the underlying agentic infrastructure.

Agent Skills 標準自 2025 年 12 月發布以來，採用速度顯著超越同期推廣的 MCP。其設計極為精簡：一個符合規範的技能包本質上是一個包含 SKILL.md 說明文件的資料夾，任何 AI 代理平台均可讀取並執行其中描述的能力。截至移交時，ChatGPT、Cursor、GitHub Copilot 等 70 餘個主流平台已完成整合，全球安裝次數接近 2.8 億次。這一數字反映出整個行業已開始圍繞 Agent Skills 作為事實標準進行建設，而非等待正式的國際標準組織認證。AAIF 下一步路線圖包括版本管理機制、安全操作指引及企業合規能力，工程貢獻者來自 Anthropic、Google 及 OpenAI 三家。Since its December 2025 launch, Agent Skills has seen adoption that outpaced MCP in comparable timeframes. The standard is deliberately minimal: a compliant skill package is essentially a folder containing a SKILL.md descriptor file that any AI agent platform can read and execute. By the time of the transfer, over 70 major platforms had integrated the standard — including ChatGPT, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot — with nearly 280 million installs globally. This scale indicates that the industry is already building around Agent Skills as a de facto standard, rather than waiting for formal international certification. The AAIF roadmap includes versioned releases, security guidance, and enterprise compliance features, with engineering contributions from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI.

對香港與亞洲數字代理商而言，這一事件的戰略意義在於「鎖定風險」的重新評估。過去，企業在選用 AI 代理工具時，最大的疑慮之一是：若主要供應商調整其生態策略，已投入的整合成本是否會付諸東流。Agent Skills 移交中立機構治理，意味著已在 Claude、ChatGPT 或 Cursor 上建立的代理工作流，未來可在更廣泛的平台上延續，不再與單一廠商命運綁定。這為代理商向企業客戶提出的長期 AI 代理部署方案提供了更穩固的論據。For digital agencies in Hong Kong and across Asia, the strategic significance lies in a reassessment of vendor lock-in risk. Previously, a key concern when investing in AI agent tooling was whether integration work would become stranded if a major vendor shifted its platform strategy. With Agent Skills governed by a neutral foundation, agentic workflows built on Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor have a more portable future — they are not tied to the fortunes of any single provider. This gives agencies a stronger case for proposing long-term AI agent deployment roadmaps to enterprise clients who are sensitive to technology concentration risk.

從實務操作角度看，Agent Skills 的極低門檻設計——一個 SKILL.md 文件——意味著代理商可以為客戶定製並封裝特定業務流程，形成可重複使用的技能包：例如封裝某品牌的週報生成流程、廣告審批工作流或競品監察排程，然後在客戶授權的多個 AI 平台上同步部署。這一能力是 MCP 所不具備的輕量優勢。隨著 AAIF 推進企業合規與版本管理功能，預計 2027 年起將有更多企業級採購流程要求供應商的代理工具符合 Agent Skills 標準，具備此技能的代理商將在投標競爭中取得明確優勢。From a practical operations perspective, the low barrier of Agent Skills — a single SKILL.md file — means agencies can package and reuse client-specific workflows as skills: a brand's weekly report generation process, an ad approval workflow, or a competitive monitoring schedule can be encapsulated and deployed consistently across multiple AI platforms. As AAIF adds versioning and compliance features over the coming year, expect enterprise procurement requirements to increasingly specify Agent Skills compatibility. Agencies that build internal competency around authoring and maintaining skill packages today will have a concrete differentiator in competitive pitches by 2027.
