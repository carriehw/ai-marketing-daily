  • Gemini 會自我介紹身份、導航語音選單、等候接聽，並可分享用戶授權的個人資料完成訂座及改期。Gemini introduces itself, navigates hold music, and shares user-approved personal info to handle reservations and appointment rescheduling.
  • 用戶可透過實時文字記錄監控全程通話，並在任意時刻接管控制。Users can monitor a live transcript and take over the call at any time during the conversation.
  • Google 以「真實對話複雜多變」為由，初期僅向美國 Pixel 11 付費訂閱用戶推出測試。Google is limiting the initial rollout to US Pixel 11 paid subscribers, citing the nuanced nature of real-world phone conversations.

報道由 TechCrunch 消費新聞編輯 Sarah Perez 於 9 月 24 日美西上午 9 時發出。技術上的關鍵一點是通話並非經由 Google 的伺服器號碼撥出，而是從用戶手機直接撥號、顯示用戶本人的電話號碼，因此商家端看到的來電與真人撥打無異。進入門檻相當嚴：除美國地區、Pixel 11 機型與付費 Gemini 訂閱三項條件外，還需安裝 Android 版 Google 電話應用程式的測試版。The report came from TechCrunch consumer news editor Sarah Perez at 9:00 AM PDT on September 24. The key technical point is that calls are not placed from a Google server number: they are dialled directly from the user's handset and show the user's own phone number, so the business sees a call indistinguishable from a human one. The entry bar is tighter than the card indicates — beyond US availability, a Pixel 11 and a paid Gemini subscription, users also need the beta build of Google's Phone app for Android.

與 Google 過去的 AI 撥號工具相比，這次的分野在於 Gemini 可在通話中提供用戶授權過的個人資料，因而能完成一整批先前做不到的動作——查門店有無現貨、把商品留貨、訂餐廳座位、改約時間。Google 對限量測試的解釋是現實對話「nuanced」，要先把體驗調順才會擴大推送。官方沒有公布訂閱層級的價格、測試用戶數量、國際擴展時間，也沒有說明非 Pixel 裝置何時支援。What separates this from Google's earlier AI dialling tools is that Gemini can now share user-approved personal information during a call, which unlocks a set of actions previously out of reach: checking in-store stock, placing items on hold, booking restaurant tables, rescheduling appointments. Google's stated reason for keeping the test small is that real-world conversations are "nuanced" and it wants to refine the experience before wider release. It disclosed no subscription price point, no tester count, no international expansion date and no timeline for non-Pixel devices.

這條產品線並非新起。2018 年 Google I/O 的 Duplex 示範已讓 Assistant 致電美髮店訂位，並刻意加入「umm」、「ahh」讓語音更像真人；2025 年 1 月 30 日推出的「Ask for Me」讓 Gemini 代打電話問價錢與服務內容；Pixel 上的「Hold for Me」與「Talk to a Live Rep」負責穿越電話樹並等到真人接聽，「Direct My Call」則直接顯示語音選單要按哪些鍵。Call for Me 是把這些零件併成一個能代辦事務的整體。競爭層面，Meta 的 Muse 與 Instinct 兩個代理已具備代撥電話能力，Google 在這一格是追趕方。The lineage is long. Google's 2018 I/O Duplex demo had Assistant call a salon to book an appointment, inserting "umm"s and "ahh"s to sound human; "Ask for Me," launched January 30, 2025, had Gemini ring businesses about pricing and services; Pixel's "Hold for Me" and "Talk to a Live Rep" navigate phone trees and wait for a human; "Direct My Call" surfaces which IVR digits to press. 數據與直覺的分寸，他的說法是「我沒有預測到這場數據革命，我一直把數據留在自己手上」，以及「可以用數據做補充，兩者都要有一點」。哈薩克航線的判斷並非來自數據——數據沒有把這個市場標示為有吸引力，理由來自跟普通旅客聊天：很多東南亞人一輩子沒見過雪，而哈薩克能比日本這類選項更便宜地提供這個體驗。他對行銷人的批評相當直接：「這個數位時代的行銷人有時太黏在電腦後面」、「他們不像我們這些老人一樣走出去理解市場」。面對輿論，他開了一場兩小時記者會，並親自到泰國見全體員工、接著在馬來西亞再做一次；社交平台上關於馬來西亞政府如何協助這家航空公司的爭論一直未停，他的原則是「你必須活在真實裡」。On the balance between data and instinct, his position is: "I didn't predict this data revolution, but I always kept all our data to ourselves," and "you can supplement it with data, but there's got to be a bit of both." The Kazakhstan route call did not come from data — the numbers never flagged the market as attractive. It came from talking to ordinary travellers: many Southeast Asians had never seen snow, and Kazakhstan could deliver that more cheaply than options like Japan. His critique of marketers is blunt: "Sometimes marketers of this digital age are stuck behind their computer too much," and "they don't go out and understand the market like we old people used to." Facing public noise, he held a two-hour press conference and met all staff in Thailand before doing the same in Malaysia; social channels remain full of argument over the Malaysian government's role in assisting the carrier, and his rule is that "you have to live in the truth."

可以搬用的部分有幾處。他自稱是 AI 的擁護者，做法是鼓勵員工用來提升生產力並給予試錯空間，而非先立一堆規矩；他偏好實體辦公，理由是純遠距或只跟機器互動會侵蝕文化與連結；在加購與簡單易用之間，他的判斷是「把產品做得太難用，就會有別人做得更好」。港台行銷人要留意七成是解決率，不等於滿意度，真正該量的是那三成如何轉人工、等待多久，以及尖峰期的降級方案。Several pieces are portable. He calls himself a big fan of AI, and his method is to encourage staff to use it for productivity with latitude to experiment rather than tight rules. He prefers physical workplaces on the grounds that remote or machine-only interaction erodes culture and connection. On balancing upsell against simplicity, his verdict is: "If you make your product too difficult, someone else is going to do it better." For marketers in Hong Kong and Taiwan the thing to note is that 70% is a resolution rate, not a satisfaction score — what actually needs measuring is how the remaining 30% escalates to a human, how long they wait, and what the degraded-service plan looks like at peak.
