對廣告代理商的類比啟示：這一現象與代理商在部署 AI 行銷自動化時面臨的問題高度類似——AI 工具在「展示」和「規劃」環節表現優異，在「執行」和「驗核」環節出現系統性缺口。特別是涉及第三方系統整合（如廣告投放平台的 API、客戶的 CRM 系統）的自動化流程，在沒有充分的端到端測試前，不應假設 AI 能夠自主完成全鏈路。Analogous insights for advertising agencies: this phenomenon closely mirrors what agencies face deploying AI marketing automation — AI tools perform excellently in 'demonstration' and 'planning' steps, with systemic gaps in 'execution' and 'verification' steps. Particularly for automated processes involving third-party system integration (ad platform APIs, client CRM systems), don't assume AI can complete the full chain autonomously without thorough end-to-end testing.

長期趨勢判斷：中國消費級 AI 工具的下一個核心攻克方向，將是如何在合規框架內完成身份核驗與支付授權的自動化——一旦這一環節打通，AI 代辦員（從規劃到執行全程自主）在中國市場的落地速度可能遠超預期。Long-term trend assessment: China's consumer AI tools' next core breakthrough target will be automating identity verification and payment authorization within the compliance framework — once this step is resolved, the speed of AI agent execution (from planning to autonomous completion) landing in the Chinese market may far exceed expectations.
