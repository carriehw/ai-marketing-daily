AI 代理從概念走向落地，但信任缺口才是規模化的真正阻力AI agents are moving from concept to deployment, but the trust gap is the real bottleneck to scale

本週多條新聞呈現同一主軸：Meta Muse 已在數百萬消費者裝置上自主執行任務；Claude Code 協調三萬個並行代理；AT&T 與 Crocs 真實測試卻仍堅守「人在迴圈」；軍事 AI 幻覺險釀事故——全部指向同一結論：AI 代理的技術邊界已大幅擴展，而信任架構（評估機制、人工監督、透明問責）才是決定落地速度的關鍵變數。Multiple stories this week share a through-line: Meta Muse is autonomously executing tasks on millions of consumer devices; Claude Code coordinates 30,000 parallel agents; AT&T and Crocs are live-testing yet holding firm on human oversight; a military AI hallucination nearly triggered an international incident.
