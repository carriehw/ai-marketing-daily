Claude Haiku 5.5 發布：API 費率最低降九成，企業批量任務成本驟降Anthropic Releases Claude Haiku 5.5 with Up to 90% API Price Cut

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • 短請求 API 費率降至每百萬 tokens 輸入 0.1 美元、輸出 0.5 美元，較前代 Haiku 4.5 降九成。Short-request pricing drops to $0.10/M input and $0.50/M output tokens — a 90% reduction versus Haiku 4.5.
  • Anthropic 估算平均可節省約七成五成本，一家客戶的任務完成延遲下降逾三成。Anthropic estimates ~75% average cost savings, with one customer reporting task-completion latency falling over 30%.
  • 新增瀏覽器與電腦操作 SDK 測試版，支援可調整推理等級，適合高量自動化管線。New browser and computer-use SDK beta added, with adjustable effort levels suited for high-volume automation pipelines.

廣告素材批量生成與品牌監測任務的 AI 成本大幅壓低，代理商擴大自動化規模的門檻隨之降低。Mass creative generation and brand monitoring automation costs drop sharply, lowering the barrier for agencies to scale AI workflows.

Anthropic 於 2026 年 10 月正式推出 Claude Haiku 5.5，針對短請求（上限 10 萬 tokens）的 API 費率大幅下調：輸入端每百萬 tokens 僅需 0.10 美元、輸出端 0.50 美元，相較前代 Haiku 4.5 降幅達九成。超過 10 萬 tokens 的長請求亦同步調降五成，落於輸入 0.50 美元、輸出 2.50 美元。此一定價與 OpenAI GPT-6 Luna 基礎費率完全吻合，並低於 Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 同等規格的公開報價，令 Haiku 5.5 在低延遲、高吞吐場景中具備明確的成本競爭優勢。Anthropic officially launched Claude Haiku 5.5 in October 2026, introducing a dramatic price cut for short requests under 100K tokens: input pricing drops to just $0.10 per million tokens and output to $0.50 per million tokens — a 90% reduction compared to its predecessor, Haiku 4.5. Longer requests exceeding the 100K threshold receive a 50% reduction, settling at $0.50 input and $2.50 output per million tokens. These rates match OpenAI GPT-6 Luna's base pricing exactly and undercut Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite at equivalent tiers, positioning Haiku 5.5 as a clear cost leader in high-throughput, latency-sensitive workloads.

Anthropics 指出，費率調降並非單純讓利，背後有 tokenizer 架構改進支撐。新版 tokenizer 對英文及程式碼的壓縮效率提升，Anthropic 估算多數企業客戶在同等文字量下可節省約 75% 的 token 消耗。金融科技公司 Rogo 實測後表示，其資料擷取類子任務的平均完成延遲較舊版下降逾 30%。這類工作流通常由 Haiku 負責文件解析、摘要與資料篩選，再將結果傳遞給 Opus 或 Sonnet 執行高層決策，分工架構使單次作業的整體成本顯著縮減。The price reduction is underpinned by architectural improvements in Anthropic's tokenizer rather than being a standalone commercial concession. The new tokenizer compresses English prose and source code more efficiently, and Anthropic estimates that most enterprise customers will reduce their effective token consumption by approximately 75% for equivalent volumes of text. Fintech firm Rogo, which uses Haiku for document parsing and data retrieval subtasks within multi-agent pipelines, reported that average task completion latency dropped by more than 30%. This pattern — delegating discrete extraction work to Haiku while Opus or Sonnet handles higher-order reasoning — has become a common architectural pattern among enterprise deployments, and the pricing change substantially lowers the per-workflow cost.

Haiku 5.5 在電腦操作基準 OSWorld 2.1 上的得分從前代的 15.7% 大幅躍升至 72.4%，幅度超過三個百分點的四倍，反映 Anthropic 將「工具使用」與「環境操控」列為本次迭代的核心研發方向。配合此能力，Anthropic 同步發布更新後的 Python 與 TypeScript SDK Beta，提供瀏覽器自動化及桌面電腦操作介面，開發者可透過 model ID `claude-haiku-5-5` 直接調用，支援平台涵蓋 Anthropic 自家 API、AWS Bedrock、Google Cloud Vertex AI 及 Microsoft Azure AI Studio。On the capability front, Haiku 5.5 registered a benchmark score of 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1 computer operation tasks, up sharply from 15.7% in the prior generation — a fourfold improvement that signals Anthropic's emphasis on agentic and environment-control functionality. Accompanying this, Anthropic released updated Python and TypeScript SDK Betas introducing browser automation and desktop computer operation interfaces. The model is accessible via the ID `claude-haiku-5-5` across Anthropic's own API, AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Azure AI Studio.

模型行為層面，Haiku 5.5 新增可調整推理等級設計，預設為「中等（medium）」推理強度，使用者可依任務複雜度在速度與深度之間自行取捨，同時加入更嚴格的網路安全預設限制，降低因自動化工具執行惡意指令的風險。Anthropic 亦同步調整 Sonnet 5.5 的快取讀取定價，從每百萬 tokens 0.20 美元降至 0.10 美元；訂閱 Max 或 Team 方案的用戶每月可獲 100 至 500 美元不等的 API 額度補貼。整體訂價策略顯示 Anthropic 正積極搶佔企業批量推論市場，將輕量模型定位為多代理架構中的執行層基礎設施，而非僅限於單一對話應用。At the behavioral level, Haiku 5.5 adds an adjustable effort or reasoning level (default: medium), allowing developers to balance speed and depth based on task complexity. The update also tightens cybersecurity defaults to reduce the risk of automated tools executing malicious instructions. Separately, Anthropic lowered Sonnet 5.5 cache read pricing from $0.20 to $0.10 per million tokens, and Max and Team plan subscribers now receive between $100 and $500 in monthly API credits. Together these moves reflect a deliberate strategy to capture enterprise batch-inference share by positioning Haiku as the execution layer in multi-agent architectures, rather than limiting it to single-turn conversational use cases.

AI 算力新創公司 Lambda 正推進一輪高達 40 億美元的融資，投前估值達 145 億美元，由 Coatue Management 與另類資產管理巨頭 Blackstone 共同領投。知情人士將此輪定性為 Lambda 上市前的最後一次私募，意味著公司預計在完成本輪融資後直接推進 2027 年的 IPO 計劃。Lambda 原本曾考慮在 2026 年掛牌，最終因市場環境不確定性而推遲，同期上市的 GPU 雲端同業 CoreWeave 與 Nebius（均有 Nvidia 股份背景）將成為其公開市場的主要比較基準。AI computing startup Lambda is pursuing a fundraising round of up to $4 billion at a pre-money valuation of $14.5 billion, led by Coatue Management and alternative asset giant Blackstone. Insiders describe this round as Lambda's final private raise before an anticipated 2027 IPO. Lambda had initially considered going public in 2026 but delayed due to market volatility; its eventual listing would place it alongside GPU-cloud peers CoreWeave and Nebius — both backed by Nvidia — as the primary public-market comparables.

Lambda 的業務積壓在短短三個月內急速膨脹，從 2026 年 6 月的 150 億美元躍升至 9 月的 500 億美元。這 350 億美元的增量，幾乎全數來自 Anthropic 於 8 月底簽署的一份雲端算力長期合約。這一結構在資本市場引發高度關注：當單一客戶貢獻如此大比例的積壓，投資人在評估積壓品質時必然會要求更高的風險溢價。Lambda 方面的立論是，Anthropic 本身擁有充足的資本支撐（包括 Amazon 的重大戰略投資），履約能力相對確定；反對意見則指出，一旦 Anthropic 削減算力預算或轉向自建基礎設施，Lambda 的收入能見度將急劇收窄。Lambda's business backlog expanded dramatically over a three-month period, jumping from $15 billion in June 2026 to $50 billion by September. Nearly the entire $35 billion increase traces to a single long-term cloud computing contract signed with Anthropic in late August. This concentration has become a central issue for prospective investors: when one customer accounts for such an outsized share of backlog growth, risk premiums on the revenue visibility discount rise accordingly. Lambda's defense is that Anthropic has substantial capital backing — including a major strategic investment from Amazon — making contract performance relatively reliable. Critics counter that any shift in Anthropic's infrastructure strategy, such as expanding its own data center buildout, would sharply narrow Lambda's forward revenue line.

從資本架構來看，Lambda 的數據中心擴張主要依賴債務融資。公司最近完成了一筆 10 億美元的增量債券，用以採購更多 GPU 機組。此次股權融資的戰略意義，除了補充工作資本，更在於向貸款機構及潛在 IPO 投資人展示股東信心，因為債務市場對 neocloud 類資產的選擇性正在收緊——部分基礎設施信貸機構開始要求更嚴格的客戶集中度測試及合約擔保條款。From a capital structure perspective, Lambda funds its data center expansion primarily through debt. The company recently closed an additional $1 billion debt tranche to acquire more GPU capacity. The strategic rationale for the current equity raise extends beyond working capital: it signals shareholder conviction to lenders and prospective IPO investors at a time when credit markets are becoming more selective toward neocloud-type assets, with some infrastructure lenders now imposing stricter customer concentration tests and contract collateral requirements.

業界將 Lambda 歸類為「neocloud」，有別於亞馬遜 AWS、Google Cloud 等超大規模雲端服務商。neocloud 的核心模式是租用或自購高密度 GPU 集群，再以按小時計費或長約形式出租給 AI 模型訓練及推論客戶，毛利結構與傳統企業軟件差異顯著。Lambda 在此賽道的競爭對手包括 CoreWeave、Coreweave 以及 Together AI 等，各家均在搶佔 Nvidia H100/H200 及 Blackwell 架構的優先部署權。隨著 IPO 時程逼近，外界對 Lambda 能否在 Anthropic 合約以外建立更分散的客戶基礎，將是決定上市定價的核心議題。Lambda is classified as a neocloud — distinct from hyperscalers like AWS or Google Cloud — operating by leasing or purchasing high-density GPU clusters and renting them to AI training and inference customers on hourly or long-term contract terms. Gross margin profiles differ substantially from enterprise software. Key competitors in the segment include CoreWeave, Together AI, and others racing to secure priority allocation of Nvidia H100, H200, and Blackwell architecture GPUs. As the IPO timeline approaches, Lambda's ability to diversify its customer base beyond the Anthropic anchor contract will likely be the central variable in determining public-market pricing.

Apple iOS 27 私設廣告科技黑名單震盪業界：The Trade Desk、LiveRamp 等遭封鎖Apple iOS 27's Secret Adtech Blocklist Stuns Industry: The Trade Desk, LiveRamp, and Hundreds More Blocked

昨日續篇Since Yesterday

10月4日報過法院裁定 Google 無須拆散廣告技術堆疊，The Trade Desk 競爭定位暫獲喘息。今日情況逆轉：Apple iOS 27 私設黑名單直接封鎖 The Trade Desk 的數據收集能力，iPhone 流量即時失去第三方 ID 支撐——法院判決保住了業界架構，Apple 卻繞過法律以技術手段重新縮窄廣告科技的操作空間。On Oct 4 we reported courts ruled Google need not divest its adtech stack, giving The Trade Desk some competitive breathing room. Today that reprieve looks short-lived: Apple's iOS 27 blocklist directly cuts The Trade Desk's data collection on iPhone — the court preserved the industry architecture, but Apple used technical means to narrow adtech's operating space anyway.

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • iOS 27 透過 WebKit 封鎖受影響廠商，由於所有 iPhone 及 iPad 瀏覽器均須使用 WebKit，Chrome 與 Firefox 與樣受波及，並非僅限 Safari。iOS 27 blocks affected vendors through WebKit — since all iPhone and iPad browsers must use WebKit, Chrome and Firefox are equally impacted, not just Safari.
  • 黑名單儲存於 Apple 私有 GitHub 存放庫，可在無需系統更新的情況下隨時擴展，受影響公司據報達數百家。The blocklist lives in a private Apple GitHub repo and can expand dynamically without an OS update, with hundreds of firms reportedly in scope.
  • Trade Desk 的廣告伺服器已從名單中移除，LiveRamp 則仍在名單之上，業界指 iPhone 用戶是「網路上最具價值的讀者」，廣告主出價能力將直接受壓。The Trade Desk's ad server was later removed from the list while LiveRamp remains blocked; industry sources note iPhone users are 'among the most valuable readers on the web,' directly compressing advertiser bid capacity.

程序化買賣高度依賴第三方 ID，iPhone 流量定向效能將下滑，廣告預算配置與受眾策略需立即重審。Programmatic buying relies on third-party IDs — iPhone audience targeting performance will fall, requiring immediate budget and audience strategy review.

Apple 於 2026 年 9 月中旬隨 iOS 27 推送了一份針對廣告科技供應商的封鎖名單，令整個程序化廣告生態在紐約廣告週（AWNY）期間陷入半公開的恐慌。受封鎖的公司包括 The Trade Desk（廣告伺服器域名）、LiveRamp、ID5、Permutive，以及 Experian 旗下的受眾資料平台 Audigent；相對地，Zeta Global 旗下的 LiveIntent、Publicis Groupe 持有的 Lotame，以及 Yahoo 的 ConnectID 則未遭波及。差異性處置立即引發業界揣測：被放行的公司普遍具備財團背書或大型媒體集團撐腰，而被封鎖的獨立身份識別服務商則缺乏同等的談判籌碼。Apple shipped a blocklist targeting adtech vendors alongside the iOS 27 release in mid-September 2026, triggering a wave of largely off-the-record alarm throughout the advertising industry at Advertising Week New York (AWNY). Companies blocked include The Trade Desk (its ad server domain), LiveRamp, ID5, Permutive, and Experian's audience data platform Audigent. By contrast, Zeta Global's LiveIntent, Publicis Groupe-owned Lotame, and Yahoo's ConnectID were left unblocked. The differential treatment immediately fueled speculation: the unblocked companies generally carry consortium backing or large-media-group affiliations, while the blocked independent identity vendors lack equivalent negotiating leverage.

此次封鎖的技術機制格外令人警惕。Apple 在 WebKit 中新增程式碼，使封鎖名單能以動態方式更新，毋須等待完整的作業系統版本更新——意即 Apple 可在任何時間點、無需通知、無需通過 App Store 審核，直接將任何新域名加入封鎖清單。IAB Tech Lab 執行副總裁 Rowena Lam 明確指出這一機制的長期威脅：動態封鎖清單令廣告科技生態難以制定穩定的技術應對方案，因為合規窗口隨時可能關閉。更關鍵的是，由於 Apple 規定所有 iPhone 及 iPad 上的第三方瀏覽器必須採用 WebKit 核心，此封鎖清單對 Safari 以外的 Chrome、Firefox 及 Edge 同樣完全生效，任何技術路徑都無法繞過。The technical mechanism behind the block is especially concerning to industry observers. Apple added new WebKit code enabling dynamic updates to the blocklist without requiring a full OS version release — meaning Apple can add any new domain to the list at any time, without prior notice and without passing through an App Store review cycle. 宏盟媒體集團（Omnicom Media Group）在 2026 年 8 月發布的研究報告《從排斥到投入：如何應對廣告迴避》，結合了兩項獨立調查：一項針對 1,136 名美國成年受訪者的問卷調查，另一項則是聯同眼動追蹤機構 eye square 對 1,200 名消費者進行的非意識層面行為測量，後者同步測試了產品植入、品牌整合與擴增實境三種創意格式的注意力喚起效果與情感反應。報告的核心發現是：65% 的美國消費者在不同程度上主動迴避廣告，其中 25% 自述屬於「非常積極」的迴避者，40% 屬「較積極」迴避。更值得注意的是，41% 的受訪者表示，付費訂閱無廣告方案的主要動機，正是為了逃離廣告曝光。Omnicom Media Group's August 2026 research report, From Tuned Out to Leaned In: How to Combat Ad Avoidance, draws on two distinct research methodologies. The first is a survey of 1,136 U.S. adults conducted in August 2026; the second is a behavioral and eye-tracking study of 1,200 U.S. consumers conducted in partnership with eye square, which measured conscious and non-conscious responses to product placement, brand integration, and augmented reality formats. The headline finding is that 65% of U.S. consumers avoid advertising to some degree — with 25% describing themselves as very or extremely active avoiders, and 40% falling into a moderately active avoidance category. A supplementary data point with significant commercial implications: 41% of respondents report that avoiding advertising is a primary motivation for paying for ad-free subscription services.

研究的方法論區分了主動與被動兩種迴避行為。主動迴避涵蓋安裝廣告攔截器、略過可跳過廣告、靜音、向下滾動，以及主動選擇無廣告訂閱服務；被動迴避則包括在廣告出現時拿起第二塊螢幕、多工處理，或對重複出現的不相關廣告進行心理過濾。前者是行為選擇，後者是注意力分配的結果，兩者的干預策略截然不同。報告的重要結論是：廣告迴避是一個「可改變的行為光譜」，而非固定的受眾屬性；消費者的抗拒源於廣告質素低劣、頻次過高或情境不符，並非對品牌本身的根本排斥。研究主管 O'Connell 的核心引言精準點出了這一張力：「消費者說品牌有許可——他們只是希望廣告更好。」The research draws a methodologically important distinction between active and passive avoidance behaviors. Active avoidance encompasses ad blocker installation, skipping pre-roll ads, muting, scrolling past, and deliberately choosing ad-free streaming tiers. Passive avoidance covers second-screening during ad breaks, multitasking, and the mental filtering that consumers apply to repetitive or contextually irrelevant messages. These two categories require different intervention strategies: active avoidance reflects a deliberate choice and demands structural solutions at the format or placement level, while passive avoidance is primarily an attention allocation problem addressable through relevance and creative quality. The report's central conclusion reframes the problem: ad avoidance is a mutable behavioral spectrum, not a fixed audience attribute. Consumer resistance stems from poor creative execution, excessive frequency, and contextual mismatch — not from an intrinsic aversion to brand communication. The study's lead, O'Connell, articulated this directly: consumers are saying brands have permission; they just want it to be good.

報告提出四項具體建議。第一，將頻次管理、情境定向與創意設計整合處理，而非獨立優化：單純限制同一素材的曝光次數，卻在不相關情境下持續投放，效果仍然有限；唯有三者協同，才能讓每一次接觸都具備相關性。第二，將廣告出現的整體體驗納入設計範疇，而非只設計廣告本身：考量廣告在什麼時機插入、對周邊內容造成多大干擾、是否為受眾提供了與收看成本相稱的價值。第三，主動採用傳統格式以外的形式，包括產品植入、品牌整合、冠名贊助以及沉浸式體驗，這些形式在眼動研究中展示出更高的非意識注意力水平與更正面的情感評分。第四，以整體戰略框架統整內容、電商、贊助、媒體與量測，避免各部門各自為政導致消費者接觸點碎片化。Four actionable recommendations follow from the research. First, frequency management, contextual targeting, and creative strategy should be integrated rather than optimized separately; capping exposures on a single creative while continuing to serve it in mismatched contexts delivers limited improvement without contextual coherence. Second, the full experience of an ad appearing should be designed — not just the ad unit itself — taking into account insertion timing, disruption to surrounding content, and whether the value exchange justifies the attention cost imposed on the viewer. Third, advertisers should actively move beyond traditional interruptive formats: the eye-tracking data showed that product placement, brand integration, and sponsored content generated higher non-conscious attention scores and more favorable emotional response profiles than standard pre-roll or display units. Fourth, a unified strategic framework should connect content, commerce, sponsorship, media buying, and measurement; siloed execution produces fragmented consumer touchpoints that compound avoidance rather than resolve it.

這項研究的行業意義在於它為廣告主提供了一個反直覺的再框架：廣告迴避的解法不是減少廣告投放，而是提升廣告值得被看的程度。41% 的受訪者付費只為逃避廣告，這一數字折射出市場願意為「無廣告體驗」支付真實金錢成本——而廣告創作者若能將廣告本身轉化為有吸引力的內容形式，這部分消費者仍存在被重新觸達的窗口。對品牌廣告主而言，最昂貴的廣告支出，從來不是高價位的優質媒體版位，而是在錯誤情境、以錯誤頻次播出的廣告所積累的品牌信任損耗。The broader implication of this research is a counterintuitive reframe for media planners: the solution to ad avoidance is not fewer ads, but ads that warrant attention. The 41% of consumers who pay to escape advertising represent a segment that has expressed a revealed preference for ad-free environments — yet the same research confirms they retain permission for brand communication when it meets a quality threshold. For advertisers, the most expensive media investment is not a premium placement; it is a low-quality, high-frequency ad running in an irrelevant context, eroding brand trust one interrupted viewing session at a time.
