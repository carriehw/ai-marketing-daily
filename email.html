Anthropic Releases Claude Haiku 5.5 with Up to 90% API Price Cut

Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 cuts API prices up to 90% for short tasks, matching GPT-6 Luna's base pricing and adding a browser automation SDK beta for high-volume enterprise use.

Why it matters: Mass creative generation and brand monitoring automation costs drop sharply, lowering the barrier for agencies to scale AI workflows.

02 — Ad Platforms & Martech
Business impact Global
Apple iOS 27's Secret Adtech Blocklist Stuns Industry: The Trade Desk, LiveRamp, and Hundreds More Blocked

Since yesterday: On Oct 4 we reported courts ruled Google need not divest its adtech stack, giving The Trade Desk some competitive breathing room. Today that reprieve looks short-lived: Apple's iOS 27 blocklist directly cuts The Trade Desk's data collection on iPhone — the court preserved the industry architecture, but Apple used technical means to narrow adtech's operating space anyway.

Apple iOS 27 secretly blocks The Trade Desk, LiveRamp, ID5, Permutive and hundreds of others via a private GitHub-hosted list that can expand without an OS update — industry at AWNY calls it an 'accelerant' for ID-based advertising's decline.

Why it matters: Programmatic buying relies on third-party IDs — iPhone audience targeting performance will fall, requiring immediate budget and audience strategy review.

Worth watching Global
pubX Raises $5M Series A to Scale Agent-to-Agent Ad Trading on Open AdCP Standard

Programmatic infrastructure startup pubX closes $5M Series A led by Chicago Ventures to build agent-to-agent media trading on the open Ad Context Protocol (AdCP), acquiring media quality firm Compliant to embed governance signals into autonomous trades.

Why it matters: Agentic ad trading is moving from concept to infrastructure — agencies need to track AdCP adoption to gauge when autonomous buying becomes operationally viable.

Google SynthID Goes Public at Synthid.com — 1 Million Daily Checks for AI-Generated Images, Video and Audio

Google launches synthid.com for anyone to verify whether images, video or audio is AI-generated — reaching 1 million daily verifications, with Apple support coming and OpenAI plus Nvidia already integrated.

Why it matters: Clients can now instantly verify whether ad creatives are AI-generated — agencies need disclosure and transparency standards for AI production.

Why it matters: Zero-code game creation opens a low-barrier path to gamified brand activations for agencies.

Omnicom research of 2,300+ consumers finds 65% actively avoid ads and 41% pay for subscriptions to escape them — with four solutions: context-creative integration, experience design, non-standard formats, and holistic measurement.
