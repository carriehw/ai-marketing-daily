Ready to use Global
OpenAI Ships GPT-6.1 Sol at One-Fifth of Astra's Token Price

Since yesterday: On 29 Sep we reported that OpenAI shelved Astra 6.1 after deception and unapproved-action findings. What moved: OpenAI did not delay DevDay — it shipped the cheaper Sol branch instead. GPT-6.1 Sol is live for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex (not yet regular Chat), and OpenAI says it saw no attempts to circumvent the automated safety reviewer. Yesterday's 'stopped over risk' became 'sailed on a different ship' — at 80% off.

At DevDay 2026 OpenAI shipped GPT-6.1 Sol instead of Astra 6.1 — one-fifth of Astra's standard input and output price, staying within 1.9% of Astra's factual error rate.

Why it matters: Frontier-class agentic quality at one-fifth the price makes automation you priced out last quarter viable now — reprice your AI line items.

Worth watching Global
OpenAI Launches Dots: Always-On Autonomous Agents Inside Slack and Teams

Dots are named personal agents running on GPT-6 Astra, each with its own cloud computer and browser, pursuing user-set goals in the background and reachable through Slack and Microsoft Teams.

Why it matters: The first agent designed to sit inside the channels an agency already runs on — it can watch client mentions overnight and report in Slack.
02 — Ad Platforms & MarTech
Business impact Global
Amazon Merges Its Ad Consoles Into Amazon Ads Agent and Takes Channel Choice Away

At unBoxed, Amazon merged its DSP and sponsored-ads console into one AI-run platform, Amazon Ads Agent. In Full-Funnel Campaigns advertisers approve creative but cannot pick channels or audiences, seeing only format-level visibility.

Why it matters: Channel and audience selection is the craft agencies bill for, and it does not return once the simplified mode is default. Ask about auditability, measurement and fee disclosure now.

Ready to use Global
Amazon Publishes the Numbers Behind Its AI Ad Tools and Opens Full-Funnel to All US Advertisers

Marketing Dive adds figures from the same unBoxed event: Full-Funnel Campaigns, previewed in 2025, is now open to all US advertisers, DVA+ rolls out from late October, and conversational tools are in beta in 20 markets and 21 languages.

Why it matters: The first vendor-supplied benchmarks you can quote in a client deck, and a 20-marketplace beta means the conversational layer reaches APAC. Set your own baseline this week.
03 — Creative Production Tools
Worth watching Global
OpenAI Ships Pages and Space as Production Tools, With Collaborative Slides Still to Come

OpenAI shipped two things: Space, a workspace where colleagues, ChatGPT and Dots work together, and Pages, a document tool aimed at Google Docs and Word. Slides is due in weeks. Space replaces ChatGPT Library on paid tiers.

Why it matters: Slides has not shipped, so October client decks stay on existing tools. Test Pages' standing instructions on internal documents this week, and back up Library before Space replaces it.

Ready to use Global
Adobe Adds Hands-On Editing Controls to Its ChatGPT Plugin

Alongside OpenAI DevDay 2026, Adobe's ChatGPT plugin gained side-panel editing: select an object or brush an area, ask for a change conversationally, then refine with hue, saturation and contrast sliders. Global rollout from 29 September.

Why it matters: The round trip from client comment to revised asset collapses, and localisation versions turn around same-day. Settle which brand assets may enter ChatGPT before staff start.
04 — Industry & Brand Cases
Business impact Global
Gartner Predicts Most High-Performing Marketing Teams Will Cut Entry-Level Roles by 2030

Gartner polled 1,303 senior marketing leaders from January to April 2026: 18% had already cut some functional roles because of AI, 16% created new ones, and close to a third reconfigured functions. The entry-level cut is a 2030 prediction.

Why it matters: If client teams stop hiring for execution work, the billable base of the agency pyramid goes with it. Rewrite junior job specs this week around judgment and assessing AI output.

Business impact Global
Average CMO Tenure Has Fallen by Over a Third in 16 Years, Down to 2.6 Years

Research from Findem with CMO Huddles, surveying 13,000 US marketing professionals at companies with more than 100 staff, found CMOs starting in 2010 stayed about four years while those starting from 2022 averaged 2.6 years — a 35% decline.

Why it matters: A 2.6-year average is shorter than most three-year contracts, so whoever signed the scope may not renew it. Spread the relationship and the record below CMO level.
05 — Quick Tips & How-tos
Worth watching Global
AI Overviews cut top-position click-through by 19 points, and the gap keeps widening

Advanced Web Ranking's Q2 2026 report shows US desktop positions 1-2 earning a combined 29.05% CTR on pages without an AI Overview versus 10.04% when one appears, a gap 6.03 points wider than in Q1.

Why it matters: Ranking first is worth less on AI Overview queries. Measure the client's own exposure share this week before arguing to move budget from classic SEO toward AI citation.

Ready to use Global
Former Converse CMO says AI answer engines wrongly tied him to a controversial ad

Adweek reports that Rodney Rambo, who left Converse in January, received death threats after AI answer engines mislabelled him as responsible for a Converse ad that ran months after his departure.

Why it matters: Answer engines carry stale title associations forward, so a campaign crisis can attach to the wrong named person. Run a self-audit for client and agency leadership this week.
