Update: ChatGPT Space rolls out to Plus with restricted sharing

OfficialWorth watchingGlobal
Since Yesterday

Our September 30 issue covered Pages and Space in production. The November 20 update expands Plus access, with sharing limits still applying.

Key Highlights
  • Recipients need viewing permission and a paid account with Space access.
  • Free users cannot open shared pages, and public links are unavailable.
Why it matters

Agencies can assess which clients can collaborate in Space and choose delivery formats around recipient access.

Suggested next step

Confirm both accounts have Space and page permission, then test whether the recipient can open a shared client brief.

Additional context

Space supports interactive pages, slides and updates from connected tools. The incremental event is Plus access rather than the first launch of all features. Space replaces Library as the file and page entry point. Start with one collaborative brief after confirming participants' eligibility.
