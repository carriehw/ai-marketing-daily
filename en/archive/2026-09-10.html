Today's signal
AI is squeezing brand reach from both ends: AI answer engines compressing organic search traffic, and AI agents completing purchases autonomously on behalf of consumers.

Three dominant stories today — Meta Muse's agentic shopping, Instacart's AI cart builder, and publishers' paid search surge — all point to the same structural shift: AI is taking over both the discovery entry point (search) and the purchase exit point (checkout) of the consumer journey. Marketers must simultaneously deploy AEO content strategies and establish brand presence within AI agent recommendation layers.
