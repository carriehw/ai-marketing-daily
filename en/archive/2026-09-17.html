Today's signal
AI agents are moving from chatbot interfaces to operating system infrastructure: Anthropic unifies its workspace, Honor and Doubao embed agents into hardware, Feishu brings agents into team collaboration. AI has graduated from tool to infrastructure.

The core signal across four sections today: AI agent integration is moving deeper into the stack. What was once a chatbot add-on is now embedded in phone operating systems (Honor MagicOS 11, Doubao Phone), enterprise collaboration platforms (Feishu 8.0), and unified creative workflows (Anthropic Claude). Meanwhile, Meta's subscription paywall and accelerating Big Tech AI ad dominance signal that access to AI-enhanced tools is increasingly platform-controlled — something agencies must factor into client planning.
