Today's signal
A simultaneous drop in AI foundation model costs and leap in agentic AI capability is reshaping both agency service delivery and how major advertisers select their partners.

Three sections today converge on a single signal: Claude Fable 5.1 cache costs down 75%, Gemini 3.8 Flash outperforming larger models, and Sand.ai open-sourcing a 100B+ video model — AI infrastructure costs are commoditizing fast. Simultaneously, GPT-6 Astra's agentic capabilities going live, combined with PepsiCo appointing Publicis based on AI integration without a pitch, provides clear evidence: AI capability gaps are becoming the decisive factor — not a differentiator — in major client agency selection.
