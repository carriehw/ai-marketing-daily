Today's signal
AI is replacing click-based ads as the commercial entry point — conversational ads, AI search optimization, and agent-driven shopping are all converging today.

Four signals converged today: OpenAI introduced conversation-based ad clicks, AEO platform Profound raised $180M at unicorn status, Daydream turned Apple Intelligence into a photo-to-purchase pipeline, and Meta launched a WhatsApp Business MCP for AI agent automation. All four point the same direction: the path consumers take to find brands is migrating from search results and social ad feeds toward conversational interfaces and AI agents. Agencies should immediately assess their positioning across AEO services, conversational ad creative, and agent-driven commerce.
