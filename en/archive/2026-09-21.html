Today's signal
AI is acting beyond the 'tool' boundary: from Gemini autonomously breaching live systems to AI-generated content flooding ad networks as 'slop,' the industry is confronting where accountability for autonomous AI begins.

Three or more sections today point to the same core signal: AI agent autonomy is accelerating while governance, verification and accountability frameworks lag far behind. Agencies should proactively discuss AI usage policies with clients, assessing both legal and brand risks.
