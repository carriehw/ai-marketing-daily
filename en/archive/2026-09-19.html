Today's signal
AI agents are moving from concept to deployment, but the trust gap is the real bottleneck to scale

Multiple stories this week share a through-line: Meta Muse is autonomously executing tasks on millions of consumer devices; Claude Code coordinates 30,000 parallel agents; AT&T and Crocs are live-testing yet holding firm on human oversight; a military AI hallucination nearly triggered an international incident. The pattern is consistent: AI agent capability has expanded dramatically, while the trust architecture — evaluation frameworks, human oversight, transparent accountability — is the variable that will determine the pace of real-world adoption.
