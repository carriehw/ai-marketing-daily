Today's signal
Agentic AI is taking over execution across the board: Google AI Max controls ad bidding, ChatGPT Images auto-generates creatives, Meta Muse completes personal tasks — AI has shifted from decision support to direct action.

Today, three sections — AI models, ad platforms, and creative tools — all point to the same shift: AI agents are moving from 'act when instructed' to 'act autonomously on your behalf.' OpenAI's data shows AI agents now deliver 3.1x human productivity. Google AI Max auto-manages search ads without advertiser input. Meta Muse books travel and sends emails for users. This marks AI's transition from tool to actor in marketing — agencies need to rethink their supervisory role in an AI-agent execution architecture.
