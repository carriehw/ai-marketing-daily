Google Ads Help  ·  26 Oct 2026
 
Google documents AI-generated review summaries in Search ads, limited to four English-language markets
 
Google documents AI-generated review summaries within eligible Search ads in English-language searches in the US, Australia, UK and India. The feature is not documented as generally available in Hong Kong.
 
WHY IT MATTERS — Ad creative can now include platform-generated review commentary. Brands should check review quality, ad suitability and market restrictions rather than assume universal availability.

Pinterest Newsroom  ·  26 Oct 2026
 
Pinterest opens selected programmatic inventory, with Google Ad Manager support in APAC
 
Pinterest announced general availability of Programmatic Deals in selected markets on October 26, enabling enterprise buyers to access selected display and video inventory through partner DSPs.
 
WHY IT MATTERS — Media planners can evaluate how Pinterest fits existing programmatic workflows, particularly for beauty, lifestyle and discovery-led campaigns.

Stagwell  ·  26 Oct 2026
 
Stagwell acquires Freestar to expand publisher monetization and programmatic supply
 
Stagwell announced its acquisition of publisher monetization company Freestar on October 26, planning to integrate its capabilities into Owned Media and Media and Commerce.
 
WHY IT MATTERS — The acquisition highlights convergence between agency buying and publisher supply. Brands should scrutinize transparency, supply paths and potential conflicts.
