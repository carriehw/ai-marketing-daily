重點摘要Key Highlights
  • 出版商普遍承認 Google 推薦流量不會復甦，積極轉向訂閱制與直接受眾連結以分散收入來源。Publishers broadly accept that Google referral traffic will not recover, pivoting to subscriptions and direct audience relationships to diversify revenue.

2026年9月中旬於佛羅里達邁阿密舉行的 Digiday 出版峰會，匯聚了路透社、《紐約時報》、《華爾街日報》、Axios、The Verge、Politico 等逾十家主要媒體高層。會議呈現出一個清晰的集體共識：依賴 Google 推薦流量的舊模式已走入歷史，且無論演算法如何調整，這條路都不會再通暢。以 USA Today 母公司為例，其 Google 推薦流量佔比已從最高峰的逾 70% 跌至約 40%，而業界普遍預期這一數字將持續下滑。出版商們正以行動而非期待回應這一現實，積極向訂閱制、電子報、直接受眾關係與多元渠道分發轉型。The Digiday Publishing Summit held in Miami in mid-September 2026 brought together senior executives from Reuters, The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, Axios, The Verge, Politico, and more than a dozen other major media organizations. The clearest consensus to emerge: the era of Google-referral dependency is definitively over, and no amount of algorithm optimization will reverse the trend. USA Today's parent company reported its Google referral share has fallen from over 70% at its peak to roughly 40%, with further declines expected. Publishers are responding with structural action rather than optimism, pivoting to subscriptions, newsletters, direct audience relationships, and diversified distribution.

AI 內容授權協議是本屆峰會討論熱度最高的新興收益管道。《紐約時報》副總裁 Adam Greenberg 提出三項授權原則：公平的價值交換、可持續的夥伴關係而非一次性付款，以及對內容用途的控制權。然而現實遠比原則複雜——授權市場高度分散，每一份協議均獨立談判，保密協議讓出版商之間無法互相比較條款，形成嚴重的資訊不對稱。微軟的協議被形容為提供「市場透明度」，相對友善；其他科技公司則各自報價、自訂框架，令一位高管嘆言這是「全新的折磨」。更關鍵的是，目前 AI 授權收益規模仍無法填補搜尋流量蒸發所帶來的廣告缺口，中小型出版商在談判桌上的籌碼更是遠遜於大型媒體。AI content licensing emerged as the summit's most debated new revenue channel. The New York Times VP Adam Greenberg articulated three licensing principles — fair value exchange, sustainable partnerships rather than one-time payments, and retained control over content use. Reality, however, is messier than principles. Every deal is negotiated individually under NDA, creating severe information asymmetry. One executive described the fragmented process as a 'new fresh hell.' Microsoft's deal was singled out for offering relative marketplace transparency; other tech companies arrived with bespoke frameworks and wildly varying terms. Critically, current AI licensing revenues remain far too small to offset the advertising revenue lost to declining search traffic, and mid-size publishers lack the leverage that large brands hold.

生成式引擎優化（GEO）是另一個從實驗室走向商業化的新方向。Future 旗下的 GEO 產品 Future Optic 已服務逾 30 個客戶並出現合約續約，核心銷售主張是協助品牌追蹤其內容在 AI 引擎回答中的曝光度與被引用次數。然而，業界對 GEO 的商業閉環仍抱持審慎態度：AI 能見度能否轉化為真實購買行為，目前缺乏可靠的歸因數據。Future 自身坦承「大型語言模型本質上仍是黑盒」，如何在可被機器讀取的同時又保持對真實消費者的吸引力，是寫作端的根本矛盾。Ziff Davis 則採取更為務實的路線，主動為品牌客戶提供「提升 AI 能見度」的服務，從原本的內容消費者轉型為 GEO 解決方案提供者，創造額外收益。Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) has moved from concept to commercial product. Future's GEO offering, Future Optic, has signed 30-plus clients and is seeing renewals. The core proposition is helping brands track visibility and citation rates within AI-generated answers. Yet the attribution gap remains unresolved: no reliable data yet connects AI visibility to actual purchase behavior. Future openly acknowledged that 'LLMs are still largely a black box,' and the tension between machine-readable content and consumer-engaging content is a fundamental editorial challenge.

路透社的動態付費牆實驗提供了一個值得行銷人關注的反直覺案例：透過機器學習判斷個別用戶應看到付費牆還是廣告，其廣告業務反而超越了未設付費牆時的表現。核心邏輯在於，通過身份驗證的忠實訂閱者比泛流量用戶更具廣告價值，「小而精準的受眾能帶來遠多於龐大鬆散受眾的廣告收益」。這對廣告從業者的啟示是：受眾品質優於受眾規模，在 AI 驅動的零點擊時代，誰能維繫高黏著度的直接受眾關係，誰就能在廣告議價中佔據優勢。Axios 更進一步，推出名為「Axios Direct」的結構化內容訂閱產品，直接以彭博終端的概念對標代理商投資機構，試圖將新聞內容商品化為機器可讀的結構性資產。Reuters' dynamic paywall experiment offers an instructive counterintuitive finding for advertisers: by using machine learning to serve either a paywall or advertising depending on the individual user, its ad business actually outperformed pre-paywall levels. Authenticated, engaged subscribers carry more advertising value than undifferentiated mass traffic. The implication for media buyers is direct — audience quality now outweighs audience scale. Axios extended this logic further by launching Axios Direct, a structured content feed product modeled on a Bloomberg Terminal concept, targeting investment firms and enterprises with machine-readable news assets. In aggregate, the post-search publishing landscape is consolidating around audience quality, licensing diversification, and structured data products — shifts that will progressively raise the value of engaged, verified audiences and reduce the supply of cheap, high-volume inventory.

當 AI 替消費者做功課，廣告還剩下什麼？When AI Does Consumers' Research for Them, What's Left for Advertising?

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • AI Agent 代替消費者蒐集與比較資訊，使傳統廣告的觸達邏輯與入口價值受到根本性挑戰。AI Agents collecting and comparing information on behalf of consumers fundamentally challenge the reach logic and gateway value of traditional advertising.
  • 品牌需要建立可被 AI 系統交叉驗證的「品牌證據」，包括評價、服務記錄與公開資訊的一致性。Brands need to build AI-verifiable 'brand evidence,' encompassing consistent reviews, service records and publicly accessible information.
  • 廣告行業的競爭重心正從爭奪人類注意力，轉移至建立機器可讀、可信賴的品牌數據資產。The advertising industry's competitive focus is shifting from capturing human attention to building machine-readable, trustworthy brand data assets.
行業洞察Industry Insight

廣告投放邏輯正從曝光導向轉為信任導向，策略與衡量方式須同步升級。Ad logic is shifting from exposure-driven to trust-driven; strategy and measurement frameworks must be upgraded in parallel.

傳統行銷漏斗的邏輯建立在一個前提上：消費者親自走過「曝光—搜索—比較—購買」的每一步，廣告的職責是在各個節點攔截注意力、製造偏好。然而 AI Agent 的普及正在系統性地拆解這一前提。當消費者直接對 AI 表達需求，由 AI 代為完成調查、篩選、比對、甚至預訂的全部流程，廣告所依賴的「入口」——搜尋、社群 Feed、電商列表——便不再是必經的流量通道，而成為可被繞過的環節。Meta 的 Muse 等 AI Agent 已進入產品測試階段，這不是遙遠的假設，而是已在發生的市場結構位移。The traditional marketing funnel rested on a core assumption: consumers personally navigate each stage from awareness to comparison to purchase, and advertising's job is to intercept attention and shape preference at every touchpoint. AI Agents are systematically dismantling this assumption. When a consumer simply states a need to an AI and the agent handles research, comparison, filtering, and even booking autonomously, the entry points advertising depends on — search, social feeds, e-commerce listings — are no longer mandatory traffic corridors but bypassable steps. Meta's Muse and comparable agents are already in product testing. This is not a hypothetical future; it is a structural market shift already underway.

這一轉變帶來的根本挑戰，在於品牌競爭的維度發生了質變。過去，廣告的核心是製造認知落差——讓品牌顯得比實際更突出、更吸引人、更令人嚮往。AI Agent 在替消費者做決策時，不會被視覺設計或情感訴求打動，它進行的是結構化的交叉驗證：品牌聲稱專業，是否有可查證的案例？品牌聲稱服務優質，用戶評價是否一致？品牌聲稱有實力，媒體報道與公開記錄是否印證？當決策過濾層從人類轉移至機器，「說得好聽」的廣告語言失去了它原本的說服力，真實、可驗證的品牌底層事實成為新的競爭要素。The fundamental challenge this creates is a qualitative change in how brand competition works. Advertising has historically operated on manufactured perception — making brands appear more prominent, desirable, or aspirational than the underlying reality might warrant. AI Agents performing research on behalf of consumers are not moved by visual design or emotional appeals. They run structured cross-verification: does the brand's claimed expertise have verifiable case studies? Does its claimed service quality align with user reviews? Do media records and public data corroborate its stated capabilities? When the decision filter shifts from human psychology to machine logic, polished advertising language loses its persuasive leverage, and verifiable underlying brand facts become the new competitive currency.

廣告門文章提出的「品牌證據」（Brand Evidence）概念，指向廣告行業必須向上游延伸的必然性。品牌證據不是單一文件或資料庫，而是散布於官網、案例庫、媒體報道、用戶評論、社群討論、合約記錄中的整體信息生態——當 AI 研究一個品牌，它整合的是這個品牌在所有公開數位接觸點上留下的全部信號，並檢驗這些信號之間是否形成相互印證的一致性。這對品牌管理的啟示是深遠的：過去各自為政的公關、客服、社群、產品部門，其輸出內容在 AI 的視角下將被整合為同一個品牌的整體「証詞」，任何一個節點的信息矛盾都可能令 AI 系統降低對該品牌的信任評分。The concept of 'Brand Evidence' introduced in the Adquan article points to an upstream expansion that advertising agencies will need to make. Brand Evidence is not a single document but an information ecosystem distributed across websites, case libraries, media coverage, user reviews, community discussions, and service records. When an AI researches a brand, it aggregates every signal across all public digital touchpoints and tests whether those signals form a mutually corroborating, consistent picture. The implication is that PR, customer service, social media, and product teams — historically operating in silos — will have their outputs integrated into a single brand 'testimony' in the AI's assessment. Any inconsistency across these signals can lower the trust score a system assigns to a brand.

生成式引擎優化（GEO）在這個脈絡下獲得了更深層的定義。不只是讓內容更容易被 AI 引用，而是確保品牌在 AI 開始調查自己之前，已經在所有相關數位渠道構建了清晰、完整、可相互佐證的信息基礎。這與傳統 SEO 的邏輯有根本差異：SEO 是讓搜尋引擎的排名算法偏向自己，GEO 是讓 AI 系統在研究過程中得出對自己有利的結論。後者更接近於公共關係與產品質量管理的結合，而非純粹的技術優化。For advertising practitioners, this trend points toward a necessary expansion of scope. Helping brands build AI-verifiable information assets — covering user experience audits, service record organization, review ecosystem management, and cross-channel information consistency — is becoming a new capability dimension that differentiates agencies. Creative advertising remains relevant, but a new upstream battleground has emerged: if a brand's service records, user reviews, and public information cannot survive AI cross-verification, even the most compelling creative may be filtered out at the Agent's decision layer before a human ever sees it.

對廣告從業者而言，這一趨勢意味著業務邊界的必要擴張。協助品牌建立可供 AI 系統驗證的底層信息資產，涵蓋用戶體驗稽核、服務記錄整理、評論生態管理、跨渠道信息一致性審查，正在成為廣告代理商差異化的新能力維度。廣告創意的角色並不消亡，然而它的上游將出現一個過去不存在的新戰場：如果品牌的服務記錄、用戶評價與公開信息無法通過 AI 的交叉驗證，再精彩的創意廣告也可能在 Agent 的決策過濾中被直接排除。競爭重心從爭奪注意力轉向建立信任，這不只是行銷哲學的轉變，更是整個廣告行業商業模式的結構性再定義。對廣告從業者而言，這一趨勢意味著業務邊界的必要擴張。協助品牌建立可供 AI 系統驗證的底層信息資產，涵蓋用戶體驗稽核、服務記錄整理、評論生態管理、跨渠道信息一致性審查，正在成為廣告代理商差異化的新能力維度。廣告創意的角色並不消亡，然而它的上游將出現一個過去不存在的新戰場：如果品牌的服務記錄、用戶評價與公開信息無法通過 AI 的交叉驗證，再精彩的創意廣告也可能在 Agent 的決策過濾中被直接排除。競爭重心從爭奪注意力轉向建立信任，這不只是行銷哲學的轉變，更是整個廣告行業商業模式的結構性再定義。

In the medium term, multi-hardware inference competition will force cloud AI service pricing downward — when TPU inference efficiency matches or exceeds GPU, providers must reflect the changed cost structure in their pricing, benefiting API consumers. Long-term, DeepSeek's DSpark framework has been demonstrated portable to non-Nvidia hardware; the tpu-megakernels repository is publicly available, accelerating cross-architecture optimization knowledge diffusion and gradually eroding CUDA's inference ecosystem lock-in.

對行銷人而言，算力競爭格局的根本性轉變帶來的是可預期的成本紅利。AI 創意工具、文案生成 API、廣告個性化引擎的使用成本，取決於底層算力的市場定價。隨著 TPU、AMD ROCm 等替代算力持續提升推理效率，競爭加劇將推動雲端 AI 服務價格下行，行銷科技預算中的 AI 工具費用存在明顯下行空間。這對於正在評估 AI 行銷投資回報的品牌與代理商而言，意味著今日的成本基準並非固定的，而是一個因算力競爭而持續演變的動態數字，現在入場者在兩至三年後將享有更具吸引力的單位成本。For marketing practitioners and ad tech buyers, the practical consequence is a credible expectation of declining AI tool costs over the next two to three years. Creative generation APIs, personalization engines, and copy production tools are priced off underlying compute costs. As competing hardware platforms drive inference efficiency upward, cloud AI service pricing will follow. The cost benchmarks used for today's AI marketing ROI calculations are not fixed — they are a declining curve, and current adopters will find unit economics considerably more favorable as the hardware competition matures.

Vector search excels at semantic similarity but fails on exact identifiers: a query for 'payment terms in contract CT-2024-0892' may surface semantically related contracts rather than the target document. BM25 keyword matching handles exact identifiers but cannot resolve semantic variants. Production systems require both signals running in parallel, with a re-ranking model dynamically weighting results based on query type. Following Amazon Bedrock's evaluation framework, final answer quality alone is insufficient — context relevance, coverage, correctness, completeness, and faithfulness warrant separate measurement, as each dimension fails through distinct upstream causes.

存取權限控制是許多RAG架構設計時遺漏的安全層。銷售員工無權查看的敏感合約，不應因為被索引進RAG而對所有用戶開放。正確做法是在檢索階段就套用權限過濾，只有通過存取控制的chunk才進入模型上下文窗口——而非先檢索再後端過濾，後者一旦過濾機制失效即引發不可接受的暴露風險。資料新鮮度同樣關鍵：索引陳舊而置信度高，是RAG系統最危險的失效模式之一，需在chunk元數據中記錄最後更新時間並定期重建索引。對行銷技術主管而言，資料治理、分塊策略、混合檢索、存取控制、分層評估這五個基礎設施層次，恰好對應RAG從「能用」到「可信賴」之間的全部距離。Access control must be enforced at retrieval time, not generation time. Filtering after content enters model context creates an unacceptable risk window. Each chunk must pass permission validation before joining the model's context window. Freshness management is equally non-negotiable: a stale index with high model confidence is among the most dangerous RAG failure modes. For marketing technology leaders, these five infrastructure layers — data governance, chunking architecture, hybrid retrieval, access-time permission enforcement, and layered evaluation — define the precise distance between a RAG system that demos well and one reliable enough to run the business.
