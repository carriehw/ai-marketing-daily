重點摘要Key Highlights
  • 跨七大市場的 Havas 研究顯示，僅 9% 的 AI 品牌體驗獲得消費者最高價值評分，大多數品牌陷入「AI 效率陷阱」，只優化了內部流程。Havas research across 7 markets found only 9% of AI brand experiences score highest on consumer value — most brands are only optimizing internal operations.
  • 高績效 AI 體驗的消費者互動率是一般水平的 4.4 倍，在教育類、產品比較等實用任務場景表現最佳。High-performing AI experiences drove 4.4x higher engagement, performing best in utility-focused tasks like education and product comparison.
  • AI 生成的網紅內容與 AI 生成廣告在所有測試場景中得分最低，顯示消費者對明顯的 AI 生成內容持保留態度。AI-generated influencer content and AI-generated ads scored lowest across all tested scenarios, signaling consumer resistance to overt AI-generated content.
