今日核心訊號Today's signal
AI 代理行動能力提升的同一週，平台、品牌與廣告主都在同步收緊邊界。The same week AI agents gained new capabilities, platforms, brands, and advertisers all moved to draw new lines around them.

Anthropic 與 OpenAI 同日競速降價發布新模型；Amazon 以未授權為由封鎖 Meta Muse 的代購功能；品牌開始在代理商合約中寫入 AI 用途限制條款。三件事合看，是同一個結構性張力的不同截面：AI 行動能力愈強，管控壓力愈大。Anthropic and OpenAI raced to release new models on the same day; Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent from completing purchases citing unauthorized access; brands started writing AI restriction clauses into agency contracts. Viewed together, these are three angles of the same structural tension: as AI gains the ability to act, the pressure to control it grows proportionally.
