Anthropic 提出 AI 安全法草案：可預見致死 50 人以上風險的模型可被法院封鎖Anthropic Proposes AI Safety Bill Allowing Court Orders to Block Dangerous Models

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • Anthropic 建議當 AI 模型被評估為可預見、即將造成 50 人以上死亡，法院可頒令強制下架或限制部署。Anthropic's draft bill proposes that courts could block or restrict deployment of AI models posing foreseeable lethal risk to 50 or more people.
  • 草案設有豁免條款，保護學術研究與低風險模型不受相同標準限制。Carveouts are included to protect academic research and lower-risk model deployments from the same standard.
  • 此舉將 Anthropic 定位為主動推動安全立法的科技公司，與部分業界對監管的抗拒態度形成對比。The move positions Anthropic as a proponent of safety legislation, contrasting with the broader tech industry's resistance to AI regulation.

Anthropic 於 2026 年 10 月 20 日向美國國會提出一份 AI 安全立法草案，核心門檻為：若一個 AI 模型被評估為「可預見導致 50 人或以上死亡」或「造成 10 億美元或以上財產損失」，法院可應商務部申請發出封鎖令，阻止該模型上線或繼續運作。觸發評估的風險場景限定於三類：協助製造或釋放生物武器、模型脫離開發商控制自行運作，以及發動大規模網絡攻擊。On October 20, 2026, Anthropic presented a draft AI safety bill to the U.S. Congress, centered on a specific numerical threshold: if an AI model is assessed as posing a foreseeable risk of killing 50 or more people, or causing $1 billion or more in property damage, a federal court may issue an order to block the model's release or continued operation. The triggering risk scenarios are confined to three categories: assisting in the creation or release of biological weapons, a model escaping developer controls, and enabling large-scale cyberattacks.

草案設計了一套獨立評估機制。評估機構須向美國商務部登記註冊，費用由 AI 實驗室支付，惟草案明確規定「付款金額不得與評估結果掛鈎」，藉此維持評估獨立性。一旦評估機構發現模型存在迫切風險，須在 72 小時內通知商務部；商務部再向哥倫比亞特區法院申請禁令。草案亦明確限制聯邦機構的權力：各機構不得自行單方面封鎖任何模型，必須經由司法程序處理。The draft establishes an independent evaluation framework. Evaluators must register with the U.S. Commerce Department, with costs borne by AI labs — though the draft explicitly states that payment cannot be contingent on evaluation outcomes, preserving evaluator independence. If an evaluator identifies an imminent risk, they have a 72-hour window to notify the Commerce Department, which then petitions a D.C. federal judge for an injunction. Notably, the draft restricts individual federal agencies from unilaterally blocking any model; the judicial pathway is the required route.

此立法方向與加州參議院第 53 號法案高度吻合——該法案採用了完全相同的「災難性風險」量化門檻，足見 Anthropic 有意推動聯邦層面的立法與州級標準接軌。Anthropic 發言人確認，草案內容源自該公司自 2026 年 6 月起向國會遊說的《先進 AI 框架》。The proposal closely mirrors California's Senate Bill 53, which used identical thresholds for 'catastrophic risk,' signaling Anthropic's intent to align federal legislation with existing state-level standards. An Anthropic spokesperson confirmed the bill reflects the company's Advanced AI Framework, which has been shared with lawmakers since June 2026.

業界對立法取態分歧明顯。Nvidia 執行長黃仁勳主張現階段無需新增 AI 法規；DeepMind 執行長 Demis Hassabis 則倡議仿照金融業監管機構 FINRA 的模式，成立獨立行業標準機構。Anthropic 的草案介乎兩者之間：保留市場自由度，同時以司法介入作為最終安全閥，針對的是極端高風險場景而非日常部署。草案能否獲得國會足夠支持，仍是業界持續關注的焦點。Industry positions diverge sharply. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has argued no new AI laws are needed at this stage, while DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis proposed an independent standards body modeled on FINRA, the financial industry's self-regulatory organization. Anthropic's approach sits between the two: preserving market freedom while placing a judicial check as a last resort for extreme, high-casualty scenarios rather than regulating routine deployments. Whether the bill can attract sufficient Congressional support remains an open question.

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • 用戶可選擇鎖定特定對話，需透過 PIN 碼或裝置生物識別（指紋、面容）才能存取，設置儲存於本地裝置。Users can lock specific ChatGPT conversations behind a PIN or device biometrics — fingerprint or Face ID — with settings stored locally on the device.
  • 功能適用於 iOS、Android 及桌面版 ChatGPT，回應用戶對共用裝置上私人對話可見性的隱憂。The feature is available on iOS, Android, and desktop ChatGPT, addressing concerns about private conversations visible on shared office devices.
  • 此功能屬可選項目，不影響現有對話的存取方式，用戶自行決定哪些對話需要鎖定保護。Locking is opt-in and does not affect existing conversations; users decide which conversations require protection.

代理商以 ChatGPT 處理客戶 brief 或策略文件時，多一層本地裝置保護可降低資訊外洩風險。Agencies using ChatGPT for client briefs and strategy docs gain an extra layer of on-device protection against accidental data exposure.

OpenAI 於 2026 年 10 月 20 日起分批向用戶推出「Locked Chats」（對話鎖定）功能，讓用戶可為選定的聊天記錄加上存取保護。鎖定方式支援四位數 PIN 碼、Face ID 與 Touch ID 三種驗證手段，被鎖定的對話記錄將從一般歷史清單、置頂對話與搜索結果中完全隱藏，並移至獨立的「Locked Chats」專區，令同一裝置的其他使用者無法輕易瀏覽。OpenAI began rolling out its Locked Chats feature on October 20, 2026, giving users the ability to restrict access to selected conversations. Three authentication methods are supported: a 4-digit PIN, Face ID, and Touch ID. Once locked, a conversation is hidden from the main chat history, pinned conversations, and search results, and relocated to a dedicated Locked Chats section — making it inaccessible to anyone else using the same device without completing authentication.

與「臨時對話（Temporary Chats）」不同，Locked Chats 屬持久保存，用戶隨時可透過驗證重新存取。在記憶功能方面，Locked Chats 維持獨立的記憶空間：即使全局記憶功能已啟用，鎖定對話中產生的新訊息亦不會更新至一般記憶庫，有效防止敏感話題內容跨越對話邊界滲漏。值得注意的是，若用戶重設 PIN 碼，系統將永久刪除所有鎖定對話、相關檔案與受保護內容，此操作不可逆轉。Unlike Temporary Chats, which disappear after the session ends, Locked Chats are persistent and can be reopened by the owner at any time upon successful verification. When the Memory feature is active, Locked Chats maintains its own separate memory pool: new messages exchanged within a locked conversation will not update the standard memory, preventing sensitive content from bleeding into the broader context that ChatGPT draws on in regular sessions. One important caveat: resetting the PIN permanently and irreversibly deletes all locked chats, associated files, and protected content.

此功能有若干明確限制，不適用於臨時對話、已封存對話、群組對話、透過分享連結開啟的對話、排程任務對話、會議錄音對話，以及 ChatGPT for Healthcare 版本。功能本身亦不提供端對端加密，不改變 OpenAI 的數據保留政策，亦不影響「協助改善模型」的設定選項。Several conversation types are explicitly excluded from locking: temporary chats, archived chats, group chats, shared-link chats, scheduled task chats, meeting recording chats, and the ChatGPT for Healthcare variant. The feature does not add end-to-end encryption, does not alter OpenAI's underlying data retention policies, and does not change the 'Improve the model for everyone' data-sharing setting — meaning the privacy benefit is limited to access on the device level, not at the infrastructure level.

此次推出涵蓋 Free、Go、Plus、Pro、Business、Enterprise 與 Edu 七個訂閱方案，屬全計劃覆蓋。OpenAI 將應用場景定位為多人共用同一裝置的情境，例如為家人籌備驚喜、處理個人私密話題，或在職場環境中保護涉及業務敏感性的對話記錄。功能推出後，用戶如有隱私顧慮，可直接在對話層面採取措施，毋須依賴帳戶層級的訪問控制。The rollout covers all seven subscription tiers — Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu. OpenAI frames the primary use case as shared-device scenarios, such as planning a surprise for a family member, managing personal conversations, or protecting commercially sensitive workplace discussions from colleagues who may share a machine.

The federal directive is widely expected to accelerate the systemic decoupling between U.S. government systems and Chinese AI technology.
