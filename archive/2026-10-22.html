Walmart 推出 MCP 代理購物閘道，Amazon 反其道全面封鎖 AI 代理下單Walmart Opens Agent Gateway for AI Shoppers While Amazon Actively Blocks Them

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • Walmart 推出 MCP 伺服器 Agent Gateway，允許 ChatGPT、Gemini 等授權 AI 代理代客下單，並設每日消費上限與隱私保護機制。Walmart launched an MCP server called Agent Gateway, allowing approved AI agents including ChatGPT and Gemini to complete purchases on behalf of users, with daily spending limits and privacy controls.
  • Amazon 採取截然相反的立場，主動封鎖所有 AI 代理自動完成購買，要求消費者親自操作結帳流程。Amazon is taking the opposite approach, actively blocking AI agents from completing purchases and requiring human checkout.
  • 兩大零售商的策略分歧預示品牌將面臨全新競爭維度：在 AI 代理推薦系統中的「可被發現性」，或成未來電商競爭的核心指標。The strategic divergence signals a new competitive frontier for brands: 'agent discoverability' — surfacing in AI agent shopping recommendations — may become the next critical optimization metric.
行業洞察Industry Insight

AI 代理商務時代到來，品牌電商廣告策略與受眾觸達邏輯需全面重新考量。Agentic commerce is arriving; brand e-commerce ad strategy and audience targeting logic will need a fundamental rethink.

趨勢觀察Pattern Watch

品牌商品能否在 AI 代理購物推薦中出現，將決定其在新型消費渠道的觸及率；類似搜尋引擎優化的競爭邏輯，可能在 AI 代理時代以新的形式重演。Whether a brand's products surface in AI agent shopping recommendations will define reach in this new consumer channel — the SEO competition logic of the past may replay in a new form for the agentic commerce era.

Walmart 的 Agent Gateway 採用 Model Context Protocol（MCP）標準，允許授權的 AI 代理——目前包括 ChatGPT 與 Google Gemini——以消費者的 Wallet 帳戶直接下單，無需消費者打開應用程式或重新輸入付款資料。每日消費上限與隱私設定由消費者自行配置，這意味著整個授權流程是明確的選擇加入，而非默認開放。Walmart's Agent Gateway uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP), allowing authorized AI agents — currently including ChatGPT and Google Gemini — to complete purchases through a consumer's Wallet account without the user opening the app or re-entering payment details. Daily spending limits and privacy settings are configured by the consumer, meaning the entire authorization flow is an explicit opt-in rather than a default.

Amazon 的立場與此完全相反。Amazon 正在收緊系統對 AI 代理自動完成購買的支援，要求消費者在最後一步親自確認。這一分歧有其商業邏輯：Amazon 的廣告收入高度依賴 Sponsored Products 的競投機制，而 AI 代理介入之後，真實的消費行為信號可能被繞過，令廣告定向的精準度下降。Walmart 自身廣告業務體量遠小於 Amazon，加之其電商業務長期追趕，開放代理商務帶來增量流量的潛在收益，高於其保護現有廣告生態的必要性。Amazon is taking the exact opposite position, quietly tightening system restrictions on AI agents completing automated purchases and requiring human confirmation at the final step. The divergence has a clear commercial logic: Amazon's ad revenue depends heavily on its Sponsored Products auction, and AI agent intermediation threatens to bypass real-time consumer behavior signals that make ad targeting precise. Walmart's own ad business is far smaller, and its e-commerce operation is in structural catch-up mode — the incremental traffic from agentic commerce is worth more than protecting an existing ad ecosystem.

對品牌來說，這是一個需要提前部署的戰場。AI 代理推薦商品時，決策依據是商品描述的清晰度、評分、庫存與定價等結構化數據，而非實時廣告競投。這套邏輯更接近搜尋引擎優化——品牌需要確保商品數據的準確與完整，讓 AI 代理在代購清單中能夠識別與推薦其商品，而不只是依賴廣告曝光。一個已在 Walmart Agent Gateway 上優化了商品內容的品牌，在 Amazon 最終開放代理商務時同樣佔有先發優勢。For brands, this is a front that requires early preparation. When AI agents select products to recommend, they evaluate structured data — description clarity, ratings, inventory, and pricing — not real-time ad bids. The emerging logic is closer to SEO than programmatic advertising. Brands that optimize their product data for AI agent discoverability on Walmart's platform today will carry that advantage when Amazon eventually opens the same door.

Nielsen 的調查覆蓋 1,700 名資深管理層，超過 50% 坦言難以評核員工 AI 輔助工作的真實品質。這一困境有其根源：當員工使用 AI 輔助完成報告、分析或提案，最終產出的文字可能更流暢，格式更整齊，卻未必比員工獨立完成更具洞察力或準確性。管理層在評核時面對的是「看似更好的輸出」，卻缺乏可靠的方法區分 AI 潤色與真實思考之間的邊界。Nielsen's survey of 1,700 senior leaders found over 50% openly acknowledge difficulty evaluating the true quality of AI-assisted employee work. The root cause is structural: when employees use AI to assist with reports, analysis, or pitches, the output may appear more polished and better formatted, yet not necessarily more accurate or insightful than unassisted work. Managers face 'improved-looking outputs' with no reliable method to distinguish AI polish from genuine thinking.

調查同時揭示一個弔詭的現象：企業的 AI 工具數目不減反增，平均每名員工使用的 AI 工具已超過三種。管理層被要求在績效考核中評估員工的「提示詞能力」，卻有 39% 的受訪高管坦言已開始用 AI 撰寫員工的績效考核——亦即用 AI 評核員工用 AI 完成的工作。這種雙重依賴製造了系統性風險：任何評估鏈條中的偏差都會被放大，而不是被糾正。The survey also reveals a paradox: AI tool sprawl is increasing rather than consolidating, with the average employee now using more than three AI tools. Managers are expected to evaluate employees' 'prompt engineering skills' in performance reviews — yet 39% of surveyed executives admitted they already use AI to draft those performance reviews. This creates a double-dependency: using AI to evaluate work done with AI, where any bias in the evaluation chain gets amplified rather than corrected.

對代理商的啟示在於：內部品質管控的框架需要提前建立，而非在客戶提出質疑時才補救。具體而言，代理商需要定義哪些類型的工作可以 AI 輔助、哪些需要人工全程，並建立可審查的審核記錄。這套機制不僅保護交付品質，也是對客戶呈現代理商作為「可信賴 AI 工作夥伴」定位的最具說服力的方式。For agencies, the implication is that internal quality control frameworks need to be built proactively, not assembled in response to client questions. Practically, this means defining which types of work are AI-assisted, which require fully human execution, and building auditable review records. That mechanism protects delivery quality and is also the most persuasive way to position an agency as a 'trustworthy AI work partner' to clients who are wrestling with exactly the same management challenge.

Forbes 加入 Google 付費計劃，意味著它選擇了「與其被摘要，不如將摘要授權賺費」的策略，以獲取 AI 引用連結、語音簡報機會及用戶行為洞察作為回報。具體交換條款未披露，但 Google 的同類計劃已覆蓋逾 400 家媒體，說明業界已形成一定的市場定價共識。這是出版商對 AI 流量損失問題，在版權訴訟之外的另一條務實應對路徑。By joining the paid program, Forbes chose the strategy of 'if you're going to be summarized, get paid for licensing the summaries' — in exchange for AI citation links, audio briefing inclusion, and user behavior insights. The specific financial terms were not disclosed, but Google's equivalent programs now span 400+ publishers, suggesting the industry has reached a working market price consensus. This represents a pragmatic path for publishers dealing with AI traffic erosion, alongside the copyright litigation route.

對品牌內容行銷與 SEO 策略的啟示在於：如果主流媒體已開始針對「AI 引用可見性」而非「點擊流量」重新優化內容結構，品牌的自有媒體內容也需要跟進相同邏輯。未來的 KPI 體系可能需要納入「AI 引用率」——即品牌相關主題在 Google AI Overview 或類似系統中被引用的頻率與準確度——而非單純追蹤自然搜尋點擊量。代理商若能提前建立對客戶 AI 引用表現的監測框架，將在下一輪 SEO 工具迭代中佔據先機。The implication for brand content marketing and SEO strategy is that if mainstream media is already restructuring content for 'AI citation visibility' rather than 'click traffic,' brand-owned media content needs to follow the same logic. Future KPI frameworks may need to incorporate an 'AI citation rate' — how frequently and accurately brand-relevant topics appear in Google AI Overview and similar systems — rather than tracking organic search clicks alone. Agencies that build client AI citation monitoring frameworks early will be positioned ahead of the next cycle of SEO tool evolution.
