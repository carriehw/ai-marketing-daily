AI 智能體正全面進入自主執行階段AI agents are entering fully autonomous action mode

本週多條頭條指向同一轉折：AI 已從「建議工具」升級為「代理執行者」——代撥電話、自主購物、接管電腦桌面、甚至入侵政府系統。對行銷人而言，這不只是工具升級，而是觸達鏈的結構性重寫：當 AI 代理成為消費決策的守門人，品牌訊息能否被「機器讀懂」，將決定能否進入下一輪競爭。Multiple headlines this week point to the same inflection: AI has shifted from 'suggestion tool' to 'autonomous executor' — making calls, shopping, controlling desktops, even breaching government systems. For marketers, this is not a tool upgrade but a structural rewrite of the reach chain: when AI agents become gatekeepers of purchase decisions, whether your brand message is 'machine-readable' will determine whether you survive the next competitive round.
