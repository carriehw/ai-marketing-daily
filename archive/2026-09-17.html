今日核心訊號Today's signal
AI 代理人正從聊天介面走向作業系統層：Anthropic 統一工作流、榮耀與 Doubao 手機把代理人嵌入硬體、飛書把代理人引入企業協作——AI 已從工具升格為基礎架構。AI agents are moving from chatbot interfaces to operating system infrastructure: Anthropic unifies its workspace, Honor and Doubao embed agents into hardware, Feishu brings agents into team collaboration. AI has graduated from tool to infrastructure.

今日跨越四個版塊的核心訊號：AI 代理人的整合層級持續下沉。年初代理人還是外掛工具，現在已進入手機作業系統（榮耀 MagicOS 11、Doubao 手機）、企業協作平台（飛書 8.0）、統一創作工作流（Anthropic Claude）。與此同時，Meta 的付費訂閱牆與廣告平台 AI 市佔加速，提示代理商：客戶接觸 AI 增強工具的管道，正愈來愈受平台所控。The core signal across four sections today: AI agent integration is moving deeper into the stack. What was once a chatbot add-on is now embedded in phone operating systems (Honor MagicOS 11, Doubao Phone), enterprise collaboration platforms (Feishu 8.0), and unified creative workflows (Anthropic Claude). Meanwhile, Meta's subscription paywall and accelerating Big Tech AI ad dominance signal that access to AI-enhanced tools is increasingly platform-controlled — something agencies must factor into client planning.
