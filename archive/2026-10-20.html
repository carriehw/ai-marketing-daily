Anthropic 近期公開了一份內部勞動力分析報告，揭示其組織架構正在發生結構性轉變。最顯著的指標，是初級技術職員佔技術部門的比例，已由 2025 年初的 21% 降至目前的 14%；若以新進初級職員佔整體招聘比例計算，跌幅更為陡峭——從 27% 急降至 9%。技術職員佔全體員工的比例亦同步收縮，由 61% 下降至 56%，主要原因是非技術職能部門擴張，以及業務人員已能直接使用 AI 工具自行完成原本需要工程師介入的任務。Anthropic's recently published internal workforce analysis reveals a structural shift in how the company is organized. The most striking headline figure: junior technical staff have fallen from 21% of the technical workforce in early 2025 to just 14% today. New junior technical hires show an even sharper decline — dropping from 27% of all new hires to just 9%. Technical staff as a share of total headcount also contracted, from 61% to 56%, driven by growth in non-technical business functions and by non-engineers increasingly using AI tools to complete tasks that once required developer support.

報告揭示的另一組數據，同樣值得香港代理商管理層正視：AI 代理（Claude）的工時遠超人類員工。研究部門中，代理與人類的工時比例達到 19:1；工程師則平均產出多出五倍的程式碼提交量，且未見任何代碼質素下滑的跡象。與此同時，2026 年 4 月一份內部問卷顯示，27% 的員工表示因使用 Claude 而顯著提升生產力，20% 的員工描述自身角色已從「直接執行」轉變為「管理多個 Claude 實例」。這一轉變重新定義了何謂「高價值員工」——能夠設計任務框架、審核 AI 輸出及承擔問責的人，正在取代純粹執行的角色。The productivity data accompanying these figures deserves close attention from agency leaders in Hong Kong. AI agents — specifically Claude — now log working hours at a ratio of 19:1 versus human researchers in Anthropic's research division. Engineers are producing five times more code commits without any measurable decline in quality. An April 2026 internal survey found 27% of employees reported significant productivity gains attributable to Claude, while 20% described their role as having shifted from direct execution to 'managing multiple Claude instances.' This redefines what a high-value employee looks like: the premium is increasingly on people who can frame tasks, audit AI output, and bear accountability — not those who simply execute.

值得注意的是，效率提升並未帶來會議時間的減少，反而相反。報告顯示，每位員工的會議時間按年上升 20%，技術人員的會議時間更增加 16%。Anthropic 的解釋是，AI 大量生成內容之後，人類的核心職責轉向信任建立與責任確認，面對面協調反而變得更加關鍵，而非更少。一名工程師甚至以「保姆」比喻自身現況——同時照看多個正在並行運作的 AI 代理，每一個都需要相當程度的注意力。Counter-intuitively, these efficiency gains have not reduced meeting time. Per-employee meeting time rose 20% year-over-year overall, and 16% among technical contributors specifically. Anthropic attributes this to a shift in the nature of human work: once AI handles the production volume, human attention becomes the bottleneck for trust-building and accountability verification, making in-person coordination more critical rather than less. One engineer described the experience as 'babysitting' — simultaneously managing multiple Claude instances, each demanding meaningful attention.

對於正在評估 AI 轉型路徑的香港行銷代理商而言，這份報告提供了一個具參考價值的早期樣本。首先，招聘策略需要重新校準：初級執行職位不會完全消失，然而其佔比壓縮幾乎是必然趨勢，未來的初級員工需要從入職第一天起就具備「管理 AI 輸出」的能力，而非單純執行重複性任務。其次，管理層需要在組織設計上預留更多橫向協調資源——隨著 AI 工具的滲透率提升，跨部門談定同一個決策、分清誰負責的成本，往往比預期中更高。For Hong Kong marketing agencies evaluating their own AI transition roadmap, this report offers a rare early-stage data point from an organization that is arguably the world's most AI-saturated workforce. Three practical implications stand out. First, hiring strategy needs recalibration: junior execution roles will not vanish entirely, but compression is near-certain, and any new junior hire must be prepared from day one to manage AI output rather than replace it. Second, management structures must budget for more lateral coordination overhead — as AI penetration increases, cross-functional decision alignment and accountability definition consistently cost more than anticipated. Third, Anthropic explicitly positions itself as an early indicator for economy-wide labor shifts; agencies that begin tracking analogous internal metrics now will be far better positioned to plan workforce strategy 12 to 24 months ahead.

在 2026 年 10 月的 TechCrunch Disrupt 大會上，Replit 執行長 Amjad Masad 公開宣布，平台已將預設 AI 模型由 Anthropic 的 Claude 切換至 OpenAI，並正式確認 OpenAI 為 Replit Agent 的「首選合作夥伴」。Replit 曾是 Anthropic 排名前五位的付費客戶，合作期間幫助其收入實現約十倍增長。這次轉換標誌著 AI 基礎模型市場迄今最具份量的客戶流失事件之一。At TechCrunch Disrupt in October 2026, Replit CEO Amjad Masad publicly announced that the platform had switched its default AI model from Anthropic's Claude to OpenAI, naming OpenAI the 'preferred partner' powering Replit Agent. Replit had been among Anthropic's top-five paying customers — a relationship that helped Replit grow its revenue roughly tenfold. This switch marks one of the most significant customer departures in the AI foundation model market to date.

Masad 給出三個理由：效能方面，內部基準測試顯示 OpenAI 模型在從零構建「vibe-coded」應用程式上表現更佳；成本方面，OpenAI 提供更具競爭力的定價；合作深度方面，他形容 OpenAI「目前是更好的夥伴」。值得注意的是，OpenAI 合作關係早於 2026 年 8 月已宣布，而 Anthropic 出局的消息卻拖延近兩個月才曝光——直至 9 月下旬，Replit 文件仍在推廣 Claude Fable 5 及 Opus 5.5，商業決策與公開溝通之間的落差令外界側目。Masad cited three reasons: performance (internal benchmarks showed OpenAI models outperform Claude when building vibe-coded applications from scratch), cost (more competitive pricing), and partnership quality (OpenAI described as 'a better partner for now'). Notably, the OpenAI collaboration was announced back in August 2026, yet Anthropic's displacement did not emerge until nearly two months later — as recently as late September 2026, Replit's documentation was still actively promoting Claude Fable 5 and Opus 5.5, exposing a significant gap between commercial decisions and public communications.

這次事件的核心啟示，在於 AI 模型供應商的黏性遠低於傳統軟件。「預設」位置才是真正的戰略資產：絕大多數用戶從不主動切換模型，失去預設等同失去絕大部分流量。對於在業務中構建或採購 AI 工具的香港代理商，有兩個實務要點：其一，評估任何 AI 平台時，應主動詢問底層模型及供應商的切換政策——按某模型能力設計的工作流程，可能因靜默替換而退化；其二，過度依賴單一模型供應商帶來架構脆弱性，保持模型層可替換性是降低長期風險的基本設計原則。Replit 企業收入佔比已由 2025 年約 2% 升至 2026 年約 15%，預計 2027 年超過 50%，亦印證企業客戶對 AI 工具供應商穩定性的關注度正在持續上升。The core lesson of this episode is that AI model vendor stickiness is far lower than traditional software lock-in. The 'default' slot is the true strategic asset: the vast majority of users never manually switch models, so losing default status means losing the bulk of platform traffic. For Hong Kong agencies building or procuring AI-powered tools, two practical points stand out. First, when evaluating any AI platform, actively ask what the underlying model is and what the vendor's policy is on switching — a workflow designed around a specific model's capabilities may degrade silently after a substitution. Second, over-reliance on a single model vendor creates architectural fragility; maintaining substitutability at the model layer is a basic risk-management principle. Replit's enterprise revenue growing from approximately 2% in 2025 to 15% in 2026 — with projections exceeding 50% by 2027 — also confirms that enterprise clients are placing increasing weight on AI tooling vendor stability.

Stripe 近期披露，其平台上的 AI 代理交易量在 9 月份按月激增 38 倍，支付金額則上升 20 倍。儘管 Stripe 拒絕公開具體的絕對數值，這組倍數增長的數據仍具有重要的信號意義——它代表代理式消費正從理論走入可量化的商業現實。目前已有約 200 個 AI 代理在 Stripe 上活躍交易，其中包括 Meta 的 Muse 及 Grok Bot 等知名實例，而 Anthropic 與 OpenAI 雖已接入 Stripe 的 Link 平台，但暫時尚未用於代理購買場景。Stripe has disclosed that AI agent transaction counts on its platform surged 38-fold month-over-month in September, with payment volume climbing 20-fold over the same period. While Stripe declined to release absolute figures, these multipliers carry significant signal value — they represent agentic commerce crossing from theoretical possibility into quantifiable commercial reality. Approximately 200 AI agents are now active on Stripe, including notable examples such as Meta's Muse and Grok Bot. Anthropic and OpenAI have integrated with Stripe's Link platform, though not yet for agent purchasing use cases.

從行業分布來看，零售佔全部代理交易筆數的 36%，旅遊佔支付金額的 28%。代理交易的典型客單價約為 100 美元，惟個別高價場景——例如機票預訂與高端家具購買——可達數千美元。Stripe 的技術架構採用代理虛擬卡號（proxy 16 位卡號）取代用戶真實信用卡資訊，並允許用戶設定消費上限，以此兼顧自動化效率與安全控制。Stripe 高管 Dan Hill 坦承，這個市場仍處於「非常早期的階段」，PayPal 及 Shopify 的 Shop Pay 均已在同一方向佈局。By industry, retail accounts for 36% of agent transaction counts and travel represents 28% of payment volume. The typical agent transaction runs around $100, though high-value scenarios — such as flight bookings and premium furniture purchases — can reach several thousand dollars. Stripe's technical architecture issues a proxy 16-digit card number rather than exposing users' actual payment credentials, with user-configurable spending limits designed to balance automation efficiency with financial control. Stripe executive Dan Hill acknowledged the space remains 'in its very early days,' with PayPal and Shopify's Shop Pay already moving in the same direction.

這組數據對香港從事零售及旅遊客戶業務的行銷代理商，有深遠的結構性影響。傳統的轉化歸因邏輯，預設消費決策的主體是人類——人類點擊廣告、人類瀏覽商品頁、人類完成結帳。一旦 AI 代理作為交易主體介入，這條歸因鏈條的根本假設便告崩塌。代理的購買行為未必源自廣告曝光，更可能源自其訓練數據中的品牌印象、用戶歷史偏好或即時比價邏輯。換言之，品牌在「代理決策層」的存在感，將逐步成為一個獨立的行銷競爭維度。For Hong Kong agencies serving retail and travel clients, this data points toward a structural disruption that will reshape the foundations of conversion attribution. Traditional attribution logic assumes a human decision-maker: a human clicks an ad, browses a product page, and completes checkout. Once an AI agent becomes the transacting party, the fundamental premise of that attribution chain breaks down. Agent purchasing behavior may not originate from ad exposure at all — it may stem from the agent's training data, the user's historical preferences, or real-time price comparison logic. A brand's prominence in 'agent decision space' is beginning to emerge as a distinct competitive dimension in marketing.

對於代理商的實際業務規劃，以下三個面向需要提前思考。第一，轉化漏斗的衡量方式必須演進：點擊率、頁面瀏覽量等以人類行為為前提的指標，對代理交易而言意義有限，代理商應與客戶共同探討如何追蹤及歸因代理發起的購買行為。第二，零售與旅遊客戶的商品資料結構化程度將直接影響其被 AI 代理選取的機率——結構清晰、資訊完整的產品資料更容易被代理解析與比較，這實際上是一個新形態的「搜尋引擎優化」課題。Three areas require forward planning for agencies. First, conversion funnel measurement must evolve: click-through rates and page view metrics premised on human behavior offer limited insight into agent-initiated transactions, and agencies should work with clients to define how agentic purchases will be tracked and attributed. 此案例為代理商與品牌主提供了一個值得深思的框架：話題跟進（Trendjacking）並非「有參與就有收益」的零成本操作。評估是否跟進時，需先考量三個維度——品牌關聯性（該話題與品牌核心主張的距離）、執行差異化（同類競品是否已飽和佔位）、以及逆向定位的可能性（若反其道而行反而能強化品牌個性）。對於定位清晰、個性鮮明的品牌而言，選擇不跟進、甚至以反向立場表態，往往比千篇一律的跟風貼文更能累積品牌資產。The case offers practitioners a practical framework for evaluating trendjacking decisions. Three dimensions deserve consideration: brand relevance (how close is the moment to the brand's core proposition?), execution differentiation (has the space already been saturated by competitors?), and the contrarian opportunity (would going against the grain actually reinforce brand character?). For brands with strong, clearly defined positioning, opting out — or even staking out an opposing stance — can generate more lasting brand equity than a generic "me too" post ever could.

總結而言，特斯拉飛行車話題所揭示的核心洞察在於：病毒式文化時刻（Viral Cultural Moment）是品牌個性的壓力測試，而非免費流量的提款機。能在同類回應中脫穎而出的，從來不是反應最快的品牌，而是最清楚自己「站在哪裡」的品牌。對行銷代理商而言，協助客戶辨別「何時跟進、何時反向表態」，正是創意策略專業價值的所在。The broader lesson from the Tesla flying-car moment is that viral cultural events function as stress tests of brand identity, not free traffic dispensers. The brands that cut through are rarely the fastest to respond; they are the ones most certain of where they stand. For marketing agencies, helping clients distinguish between when to trendjack and when to take a contrarian position is precisely where strategic creative value lies.
