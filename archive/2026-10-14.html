美國運通（American Express）於2026年10月13日正式推出「意圖智能框架」（Intent Intelligence Framework），作為其4月發布的 ACE（Agentic Commerce Experiences）開發工具套件的核心安全層。此舉旨在系統性應對大型語言模型固有的「非確定性行為」所帶來的商業交易風險——AI代理可能在執行任務過程中偏離用戶原意，例如在用戶指定購買Nike球鞋時，代理卻下單了Adidas。American Express officially launched its 'Intent Intelligence Framework' on October 13, 2026, as the security core of its ACE (Agentic Commerce Experiences) Developer Toolkit, which was first introduced in April 2026. The initiative directly addresses the nondeterministic behavior inherent in large language models — a risk where AI agents can deviate from user instructions during task execution, such as purchasing Adidas shoes when Nike was specified.

該框架採用四步驟機制運作。首先，「意圖建立API」（Create Intent API）記錄用戶的購買指令，包含商家名稱、預算上限及商品偏好等約束條件；其次，用戶通過身份驗證確認所授權的意圖；第三步生成唯一的意圖識別碼（Intent ID）及意圖證明令牌（Proof of Intent Token）；最後，系統於結帳環節以安全數字支付憑證取代實體卡號，全程不暴露真實帳戶資訊。The framework operates through a four-step mechanism. First, the Create Intent API captures the user's purchase instructions along with defined constraints — merchant name, budget ceiling, and product preferences. The user then authenticates and approves the captured intent. The system then generates a unique Intent ID and a Proof of Intent Token. Finally, at checkout, a secure digital payment credential replaces the actual card number, ensuring the real account is never exposed.

美國運通副總裁 Hannah Bozian 解釋，這套設計的關鍵在於「將意圖捕獲提前至交易流程的更早期」，從而在資金流出前就攔截偏差行為。與其他事後爭議處理機制相比，此框架屬於主動防禦性架構，而非被動補救。Bozian 亦明確表示，目前代理商業所帶來的爭議申訴量尚未見明顯上升，現階段措施具有預防性質。Amex VP Hannah Bozian explained that the key design principle is to 'pull intent earlier into the transaction flow,' intercepting deviations before funds move rather than managing disputes afterward. Unlike reactive chargeback mechanisms, this framework is architecturally proactive. Bozian also noted that dispute volume from agentic commerce has not yet risen noticeably — making the current rollout a preventive measure rather than a crisis response.

美國運通的 Trendex 消費者調查數據顯示，未來12個月內，66%的美國消費者可能使用AI代理進行比價研究，61%可能用於商品推薦。這一消費者信心基礎使美國運通認為，代理商業即將快速普及，提前建立授權標準至關重要。美國運通表示，希望此框架能成為整個行業處理AI驅動交易的通用標準，並已向開發者開放 ACE 工具套件接入。Amex's Trendex consumer survey found that within the next 12 months, 66% of U.S. consumers are likely to use AI agents for price research and 61% for product recommendations. This consumer appetite signals that agentic commerce is approaching rapid mainstream adoption, making pre-emptive authorization standards strategically important. American Express has stated its intent to position the framework as an industry-wide standard for AI-powered transactions, with the ACE toolkit now open to developers.

Google於2026年10月13日發布第14期Demand Gen Drop，推出數項以「數據強度」（Data Strength）為核心概念的功能更新，核心目標是讓廣告主能夠量化其第一方數據配置對廣告成效的實際貢獻，並將這套數據基礎設施延伸至更廣泛的Google生態系平台。Google published its 14th Demand Gen Drop on October 13, 2026, releasing several updates centered on the concept of 'data strength' — with the core objective of enabling advertisers to quantify the actual contribution of their first-party data configuration to campaign performance, and extending that data infrastructure framework to a broader set of Google ecosystem platforms.

最具代表性的新功能是「數據強度提升指標」（Data Strength Uplift Metric），即將在Google Ads介面中上線。這項指標將直接向廣告主顯示，其當前的數據配置——包括轉換追蹤、客戶比對（Customer Match）及增強型轉換（Enhanced Conversions）等設置——在Demand Gen廣告系列中帶來了多少額外轉換提升，使廣告主能夠以更直觀的方式評估數據投資的回報。配套發布的還有更新版《數據強度最佳實踐指南》，提供可操作的配置步驟。The most substantive new feature is the Data Strength Uplift Metric, set to launch in the Google Ads interface. This metric will show advertisers directly how much additional conversion uplift their current data configuration — including conversion tracking, Customer Match, and Enhanced Conversions setup — is generating within Demand Gen campaigns, giving them a more intuitive way to evaluate the return on their data investments. A refreshed Data Strength Best Practices Guide with actionable configuration steps was released alongside the feature.

在基礎設施層面，Google宣布將Data Manager功能擴展至Display & Video 360（DV360）及Google Analytics，增強型轉換亦將隨後在這兩個平台上線。此前Data Manager主要在Google Ads原生環境中提供，此次擴展意味著廣告主可在程序化購買與分析平台中使用相同的第一方數據管理框架。Google內部分析數據（以2026年6月3日至17日為研究窗口）顯示，採用Google Tag Gateway的廣告主在Demand Gen廣告系列中平均可見超過20%的轉換提升。At the infrastructure level, Google announced the expansion of Data Manager to Display & Video 360 (DV360) and Google Analytics, with Enhanced Conversions also coming to both platforms in the near term. Previously, Data Manager was primarily available within the native Google Ads environment; this expansion means advertisers can apply the same first-party data management framework within programmatic buying and analytics platforms. Google internal analysis — using a study window of June 3 to 17, 2026 — found that advertisers using Google Tag Gateway see an average of over 20% conversion uplift in Demand Gen campaigns.

美國保險比價平台The Zebra提供了一個具體的實際案例：在實施數據強度優化後，該平台實現了16%的轉換量增長，以及19%的整體轉換價值增長。這兩項數字成為Google此次更新的主要對外背書依據。整體而言，此次更新的方向是將第一方數據配置從技術合規事項重新定義為可量化的廣告績效槓桿。U.S. insurance comparison platform The Zebra provided a concrete real-world reference point: after implementing data strength optimizations, the platform achieved a 16% increase in conversion volume and a 19% increase in overall conversion value. Both figures serve as the primary external proof points for Google's update announcement. Taken together, the direction of this update is to reframe first-party data configuration from a technical compliance matter into a quantifiable performance lever within advertising campaigns.
