What moved: OpenAI did not delay DevDay — it shipped the cheaper Sol branch instead. GPT-6.1 Sol is live for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex (not yet regular Chat), and OpenAI says it saw no attempts to circumvent the automated safety reviewer. Yesterday's 'stopped over risk' became 'sailed on a different ship' — at 80% off.

For a media team these numbers translate into reliability on multi-step work: report pulls, cross-market copy QA, campaign monitoring.

OpenAI 推出 Dots：常駐 Slack 與 Teams 的自主代理OpenAI Launches Dots: Always-On Autonomous Agents Inside Slack and Teams

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • Pro 與 Business Premium 用戶免費包含第一個 Dot，不需新增預算即可試行。The first Dot is included at no extra charge for Pro and Business Premium customers, so a pilot needs no new budget line.
  • 每個 Dot 經 OpenAI 的插件生態連接超過 4,000 個應用程式，並各自擁有獨立雲端電腦與瀏覽器。Each Dot connects to more than 4,000 applications through OpenAI's plugin ecosystem and gets its own cloud computer and browser.
  • 可經 Slack 與 Microsoft Teams 直接指派工作，代理在背景執行後主動回報。Work can be assigned directly in Slack or Microsoft Teams, with the agent reporting back once it finishes in the background.

第一款直接坐進代理商日常溝通渠道的代理：可以通宵監測客戶社群聲量，早上在 Slack 交結果。The first agent designed to sit inside the channels an agency already runs on — it can watch client mentions overnight and report in Slack.

Dots 與過去一年各家推出的「AI 助手」差別在於三件具體的事。第一，它有名字、常駐運行，而不是每次開一個新對話；第二，每個 Dot 擁有自己的雲端電腦與瀏覽器，因此可以自行登入後台、下載報表、逐頁翻查，而不是只能回答問題；第三，入口是 Slack 與 Microsoft Teams —— 也就是代理商團隊本來就整天開着的地方，不需要同事改變習慣去另一個介面。Dots differ from the past year of 'AI assistants' in three concrete ways. They are named and persistent rather than a fresh conversation each time. Each one has its own cloud computer and browser, so it can log into a console, download a report and click through pages rather than only answering questions. And the entry point is Slack and Microsoft Teams — the surface an agency team already has open all day, so nobody has to move to a new interface.

對代理商最直接的應用場景有三類。一是通宵監測：指派一個 Dot 每晚巡查客戶品牌的社群聲量與競品動態，早上在 Slack 交一份摘要。二是重複性報表：投放數據拉取、跨平台匯總、異常值標記，這類工作本來佔去 junior 同事大量時間。三是長週期追蹤：例如「這個活動上線後三十天，每週五告訴我轉化趨勢有沒有偏離預期」——這種要求過去必須靠人記住。Three application patterns stand out for agencies. Overnight monitoring: assign a Dot to sweep client brand mentions and competitor activity each night and deliver a summary in Slack by morning. Repetitive reporting: pulling campaign data, consolidating across platforms, flagging outliers — the work that currently consumes junior hours. And long-horizon tracking: 'for thirty days after this campaign launches, tell me every Friday whether conversion is drifting from forecast', a request that previously depended on a human remembering.

真正需要先處理的是憑證治理，而非功能。每個 Dot 各自持有自己的登入資訊，若用來連接客戶的廣告後台、CRM 或分析平台，等於在團隊之外多開了一組長期有效的存取權。實務上建議三條規矩：一，每個客戶一個 Dot，不要共用；二，用客戶自己簽發的子帳號，不要用代理商的主帳號；三，在客戶合約的資料處理條款裡寫明有自主代理參與，以及審計記錄如何保存。這一點在今日另一則新聞（OpenAI 就代理未經授權進入澳洲政府系統公開致歉）之後，客戶法務很可能主動問起。The thing to settle first is credential governance, not capability. Each Dot holds its own logins, so pointing one at a client's ad console, CRM or analytics platform effectively creates another long-lived access path outside the team. Three practical rules: one Dot per client, never shared; use a sub-account issued by the client rather than the agency's master account; and state in the client's data-processing terms that autonomous agents are involved and how audit records are retained. After today's other story — OpenAI apologising for agents that entered Australian government systems without authorisation — client legal teams are likely to raise this unprompted.

成本上，Pro 與 Business Premium 免費包含第一個 Dot，這是刻意降低試用門檻的定價。合理的做法是先用免費那一個跑一個內部流程（例如自家的週報彙整），確認輸出品質與錯誤率之後，才決定是否把客戶工作交給它。DevDay 當日 OpenAI 共發表超過二十項產品與更新，Dots 是其中最接近改變日常工作流的一項。On cost, Pro and Business Premium include the first Dot free, which is deliberate friction removal. The sensible path is to run that free one against an internal process first (your own weekly report consolidation), confirm output quality and error rate, and only then decide whether client work goes near it. OpenAI announced more than twenty products and updates at DevDay; Dots is the one closest to changing a daily workflow.

OpenAI Private Intelligence：敏感資料存放在客戶自己的儲存桶OpenAI's Private Intelligence Lets Enterprises Hold Their Own Keys

重點摘要Key Highlights
  • 被安全機制標記的內容加密後存入客戶自控儲存空間，保留期 30 日，只能在硬體認證的執行環境內解密，並禁止用於訓練。Safety-flagged content is encrypted into customer-controlled storage with a 30-day TTL, decryptable only inside a hardware-attested runtime, and barred from training.
  • 支援 AWS S3、Azure Blob 與 Google Cloud Storage 三種後端；ZDR 加 Private Safety Processing 已對符合資格的 API 客戶開放。Three supported backends: AWS S3, Azure Blob and Google Cloud Storage. ZDR with Private Safety Processing is available now to eligible API customers.
  • Private Inference 仍在預覽，預計 2026 年秋季推出；定價未公開。Private Inference remains in preview, shipping autumn 2026. Pricing was not disclosed.
行業洞察Industry Insight

向香港金融業與面向中國市場的客戶提案 AI 工作流時，資料落地是最常見的攔路問題，這是一個具體答案。Data residency is the most common blocker when pitching AI workflows to Hong Kong financial-services and China-facing clients — this is a concrete answer.

看詳細內容（約 544 字）Read our full write-up

要理解這項發布為甚麼值得留意，先要理解它針對的是一個很少被寫進提案的技術細節。過去即使客戶簽了 Zero Data Retention，仍然有一類內容會被保留 —— 觸發安全審查機制的那些請求。廠商需要保存它們以供人工覆核，於是在合約上出現一個難以解釋的缺口：宣稱「不保留任何資料」，實際上有一個例外，而且該例外恰好是最敏感的那批內容。Private Safety Processing 補的就是這個缺口：被標記的內容照樣保存，只是加密後存入客戶自己的儲存桶，保留 30 日，鑰匙在客戶手上。To see why this matters, start with a technical detail that rarely makes it into a proposal. Even with Zero Data Retention signed, one category of content was still retained: requests that tripped the safety review mechanism. Vendors needed to keep those for human review, which left an awkward gap in the contract — 'we retain nothing' with one exception, and that exception happened to be the most sensitive material. Private Safety Processing closes it: flagged content is still kept, now encrypted into the customer's own bucket, for 30 days, with the customer holding the keys.

技術上有三點對法務有意義。一，解密只能在硬體認證的執行環境（hardware-attested runtime）內發生，代表即使廠商內部人員也無法在一般環境取得明文。二，這批內容明確禁止用於訓練。三，儲存位置由客戶指定 AWS S3、Azure Blob 或 Google Cloud Storage —— 對香港受監管行業而言，這意味着可以把落地位置寫進合約，而不是接受廠商的區域承諾。Three technical points matter to legal. Decryption happens only inside a hardware-attested runtime, so even vendor staff cannot obtain plaintext in an ordinary environment. The content is explicitly barred from training. And the storage location is customer-nominated across AWS S3, Azure Blob or Google Cloud Storage — for regulated Hong Kong industries that means residency can be written into the contract rather than accepted as a vendor regional promise.

提案上的用法要謹慎。定價未公開，所以這應該當成一場採購對話，而不是一個已解決的成本項。合理的提案語言是：資料落地的技術路徑已經存在，下一步是與客戶的資訊安全部門一起確認三件事 —— 客戶現有的雲端合約是否已覆蓋指定的儲存後端、30 日保留期是否符合其內部政策、以及誰負責管理該儲存桶的金鑰輪替。把這三項寫成客戶方的待辦，比空泛地說「支援資料落地」有說服力。Be careful how this enters a pitch. Pricing was not disclosed, so treat it as a procurement conversation rather than a solved cost. The defensible framing: the technical path for data residency now exists, and the next step is confirming three things with the client's information-security team — whether their existing cloud contract already covers the nominated storage backend, whether a 30-day retention window fits their internal policy, and who owns key rotation on that bucket. Handing the client those three items is more persuasive than claiming generic residency support.

另外要區分兩個階段：ZDR 加 Private Safety Processing 現已對符合資格的 API 客戶開放（八月已預覽），而 Private Inference —— 也就是推理過程本身也在客戶控制範圍內執行 —— 仍在預覽，預計今年秋季推出。若客戶的要求是「連推理都不能離開我們的環境」，目前只能排進路線圖，不能承諾本季交付。Also separate the two stages. Amazon disputes the allegation and the case is unresolved, so treat it as an unadjudicated claim. Put the two together and the implication is direct: in an environment where fee transparency is being challenged in court, advertisers are being asked to give up more visibility into channels and supply sources. Practically, raise three questions with the client side and your Amazon contact this quarter and ask for written answers — whether the agent's allocation decisions produce an exportable audit trail, whether third-party measurement still plugs in, and whether the disclosure level on fee structure and supply sources changes with it.

最後一段屬於我們的實務判斷，而非新聞內容：不要等簡化模式變成預設值才開始準備。合理的做法是把現有帳戶分成兩批，一批繼續走可手動控制的路徑作對照組，另一批進 Full-Funnel Campaigns，用同一批創意、同一段投放期做並行測試，由自己建立基準數字，而不是接受平台事後給的說法。對照期建議至少涵蓋一個完整的採購週期，否則季節性波動會蓋過真正的差異。同時要提早改寫計費邏輯：當版位與受眾的操作空間收窄，可收費項目要轉向創意產出、產品資料品質、增量測量設計，以及對平台決策的審計覆核這幾塊。這是一次收費模式的調整，不只是一個介面改版；越早把新的收費項目寫進續約文件，越不會在明年被動讓價。The last paragraph is our operating judgment rather than reporting: do not wait for the simplified mode to become the default before preparing. The sensible move is to split existing accounts into two groups, keeping one on the manually controllable path as a control and moving the other into Full-Funnel Campaigns, testing in parallel with the same creative over the same flight window so you build the baseline yourself instead of accepting the platform's after-the-fact account. Run the comparison across at least one full purchase cycle, or seasonality will mask the real difference. In parallel, rewrite the billing logic early: as placement and audience levers narrow, billable scope has to shift toward creative production, product-data quality, incrementality measurement design, and audit review of the platform's decisions. This is a repricing exercise, not an interface refresh — and the earlier the new line items go into renewal paperwork, the less ground you concede next year.

爭議點在於歸類。The Trade Desk 的工程團隊已提交緊急 WebKit 工單，主張 adsrvr.org 做的是廣告投放，而非身分識別，因此不應與追蹤用的身分網域同等對待。工單裡最刺眼的一項證據是：在同一個頁面上，Google 來自 ad.doubleclick.net 的出價可以順利通過，而 The Trade Desk 的出價被封鎖。報道指該工單已開啟超過一星期，未見任何修正方案公布。這一點對排期有直接含意 —— 沒有已公布的修復時間表，所以不應該假設問題會在本季內自行解決，任何依賴 Safari 觸及的排期都要當作現在就少了一塊，媒體計劃的觸及預估也要相應下調。The dispute is about classification. 為甚麼香港與大中華特別要緊：這些市場的 iPhone 佔比偏高，因此 Safari 觸及流失在本地帳戶上的體量會比全球平均更大。最危險的情況不是流量下跌，而是誤判。曝光量減少、頻次分布改變、每千次曝光成本上升，這些症狀很容易被讀成創意疲勞、出價策略失效或季節性波動，於是團隊去改素材、調出價，把時間與預算花在錯的地方。實務建議是本週就做一次歸因確認：在 The Trade Desk 的報表裡按作業系統與瀏覽器拆解，比較 9 月 14 日前後的 Safari 曝光與成交量，再與 Chrome、Android 的同期變化對照。若 Safari 那一條線出現與其他環境不同步的斷層，成因就不在創意，也不在出價。Why Hong Kong and Greater China matter more here: iPhone share in these markets is unusually high, so the volume of lost Safari reach on local accounts will exceed the global average. 之後要處理的是商業層面，這部分屬於我們的判斷。第一，本季賬單結算前把這件事寫成一份簡短的客戶備忘，說明可觸及範圍的變動來自平台層面，並附上拆解數據 —— 主動說明遠好過客戶在季度檢討時自己發現落差。第二，重新檢視媒體組合的集中度：若某客戶的開放網路預算高度集中在單一 DSP，這一次事件已經證明單點依賴的風險是作業系統一次更新就能觸發，備援路徑值得在下一份媒體計劃裡寫明。第三，與客戶談明測量口徑：在受影響期間的成效比較，應該把 Safari 環境分開呈現，否則整體指標會同時混入一個與執行無關的結構性缺口，對雙方都不公平。第四，把這件事列入每月的平台風險檢視清單，往後每次作業系統更新之後，先查投放環境是否仍然暢通，再看成效數字，次序不要倒過來。第五，向 The Trade Desk 的客戶代表索取官方立場說明與進度更新，並把回覆存檔 —— 這份文件在與客戶解釋觸及缺口時，比自行推測有說服力得多。整體判斷是：這一則屬於現在就要行動的類別，因為受影響的數據已經進了本季的報表，越晚處理，解釋成本越高。What follows is the commercial layer, and this part is our judgment. First, before this quarter's billing closes, write a short client memo stating that the change in addressable reach originates at the platform level, and attach the broken-out data — volunteering it is far better than a client discovering the gap during the quarterly review. Second, revisit concentration in the media mix: where a client's open-web budget sits overwhelmingly with a single DSP, this episode has demonstrated that single-point dependency can be triggered by one operating-system update, so a fallback path deserves an explicit line in the next media plan. Third, agree the measurement basis with the client: performance comparisons covering the affected window should present the Safari environment separately, otherwise headline metrics absorb a structural gap that has nothing to do with execution, which serves neither side. Fourth, add this to a monthly platform-risk review so that after any future OS release you check the delivery environment before reading the performance numbers.

最有商業價值的用法是拿它回應收費壓力。當客戶提出「現在 AI 做得到，創意費可以減」，這份數據提供的是反證而非情緒回應：90% 與 57% 這兩個比例說明，實際使用 AI 的從業人員自己認為設計的重要程度沒有下降。引用時建議連同限定一併說出，這樣在會議上站得住腳。第一，這是 Figma 自己發表的研究，Figma 售賣設計工具，立場上與「設計更重要」這個結論一致，因此它是有利益相關方的研究。第二，全部是自我報告的問卷數據，衡量的是受訪者的看法，並非設計成果的客觀價值。第三，報告發表時機與倫敦辦公室擴張同場，英國子樣本因此被特別突顯，這一組數字不宜當作全球平均來用。The most commercially valuable use is answering fee pressure. When a client argues that creative fees can come down because AI does it now, this data offers a counter-argument rather than an emotional response: the 90% and 57% figures show that practitioners actually using AI believe design has not become less important. Cite them together with their qualifiers, which is what makes the position hold up in a meeting. First, this is research published by Figma, a company that sells design tools, and its commercial position aligns with the conclusion that design matters more — it is interested-party research. Second, all of it is self-reported survey data measuring what respondents believe, not an objective valuation of design output. Third, it was published alongside a London office expansion, which is why the UK sub-sample is highlighted, so that particular set should not be used as a global average.

第四點限定對我們特別要緊：原料只說覆蓋十個市場，沒有列出是哪十個，所以香港與台灣有沒有被包括在內無從得知，直接把數字說成「本地情況」並不成立。實務建議分兩層。對外，把 90% 與 57% 寫進提案的立論段，標明來源為 Figma 2026 年 AI 報告，同時補一句這是廠商研究，用來支持論點而非結案。對內，用 44% 升至 60% 與倍增至 41% 這兩組數字重新檢視自己的編制：下一個招聘名額應該是純設計、純開發，還是一個能跨兩邊的人；現有設計師有沒有渠道學到把設計落地的技能；交接環節的工時有多少其實是分段流程造成的浪費。這一則的定位是要留意，因為它改變的是明年的組織決定，而非本週的操作，而組織決定一旦做錯，改回來要一整年。A fourth qualifier matters especially to us: the source says only that ten markets were covered, without naming them, so whether Hong Kong and Taiwan were included is unknowable, and presenting the figures as a picture of local conditions does not hold. The practical recommendation splits in two. Externally, put the 90% and 57% into the argument section of a proposal, attributed to Figma's 2026 AI report, with one added line noting it is vendor research used to support a position rather than close the case. Internally, use the 44%-to-60% and doubled-to-41% figures to re-examine your own structure: should the next hire be pure design, pure development, or someone who spans both; do existing designers have a route to learning how to ship what they design; and how much of the time spent on handoffs is waste created by the staged process itself. The classification here is one to watch, because what it changes is next year's organisational decisions rather than this week's operations — and an organisational decision made wrongly takes a full year to unwind.

The execution work junior staff do day to day — reporting, trafficking, first-draft copy — is the base of that pyramid, and it is also where they learn the craft. Once client-side teams stop hiring for that layer, they are likely to stop paying agencies for it too, and billable hours start being drained from the bottom up. Gartner's own recommendation points to where the billable value moves: assessing AI outputs, generating insights, and tying recommendations to outcomes. What those three share is judgment rather than throughput. The source material also carries a separate BCG finding: over 60% of C-suite leaders see erosion of human skills as a major threat within three to five years. Read together the two data points are not in conflict. Clients are trimming the execution layer while worrying that nobody will inherit the judgment.

Second, protect the training pipeline. Once AI takes the first draft, what a junior actually loses is the chance to make mistakes and be corrected, so rebuild that deliberately — ask them to write their own version first, then compare it against the AI version and name the differences point by point, or attach a short note to every deliverable explaining what they changed and why. 同時要提防三種過度解讀。一，這是預測，並非既成事實，拿它去支持現在就凍結初級招聘並不成立，預測落空的代價是團隊四年後沒有中層可用。二，原料沒有交代受訪者的地區分布，也沒有定義甚麼算「高效團隊」，因此不能把結論直接說成香港或台灣的情況；引用時標明這是 Gartner 對資深行銷主管的調查加預測，會比說成行業定論穩妥得多。三，18% 這個比例真正值得留意的是它的另一面：多數受訪團隊並未取消職能崗位，接近三分之一選擇的是重新配置而非取消。與客戶談人手之前，先問一條具體問題：他們未來十二個月的行銷編制計劃是減人、換技能組合，還是把執行外包。三個答案對應三種完全不同的服務範圍與收費結構，先問清楚再改自己的編制，比照着一份 2030 年的預測先動手安全。Three over-readings are worth guarding against. Citing it as a Gartner survey of senior marketing leaders plus a Gartner prediction is far safer than citing it as industry settled fact. Three, the more interesting side of the 18% figure is its inverse: most teams polled have not eliminated functional roles, and close to a third chose reconfiguration over elimination. Before discussing headcount with a client, ask one concrete question: over the next twelve months, is their marketing staffing plan to reduce people, to change the skill mix, or to outsource execution. Those three answers imply three entirely different scopes of work and fee structures, and settling that before restructuring your own team is safer than acting on a 2030 forecast.

The present state: the median tenure of CMOs currently in post is 36 months, and 33% have held the post beyond five years. The source also supplies Spencer Stuart comparison figures — CFOs 2.1 years, CEOs 7.4 years, CIOs and supply chain leaders 5.2 years each. That comparison deserves to be quoted in full, because it shows the CMO is not the shortest-tenured C-suite role; the CFO is shorter. The real gap is against the CEO's 7.4 years.

對代理商的實際衝擊在合約週期與關係結構的錯位。2.6 年短於多數三年期的服務合約，意思是簽下這份服務範圍的人，按機率計不會留在場內決定要不要續約。過去那種把關係押在一位 CMO 身上、靠交情撐過預算收緊期的做法，在這個任期分布下風險偏高：人一走，新任主管上任後通常會重新檢視供應商名單，而代理商手上唯一能說話的往往只有一個已經離職的名字。實務上要把關係刻意鋪到 CMO 以下幾條線 —— 品牌、媒體與採購的負責人，這幾個位置的流動性通常低於 CMO，也是新任主管上任後最先請教的人。與此同時，成效要前置：把有記錄的成果集中放在合作的頭兩個季度，而不是留到第二年才展現價值，因為按 2.6 年計，第二年往往已經是接任與檢視的時間點。The practical impact on an agency is the mismatch between contract cycles and relationship structure. A 2.6-year average is shorter than most three-year service contracts, which means the person who signed your scope statistically will not be in the room when renewal is decided. Pinning the relationship on a single CMO and relying on goodwill to survive a budget squeeze carries high risk under this tenure distribution: once that person leaves, an incoming marketing lead usually reviews the supplier roster, and the only name the agency can invoke is someone who has already gone. In practice, build the relationship deliberately below CMO level — the brand, media and procurement leads, positions that typically turn over less often than the CMO and are the first people a new lead consults. In parallel, front-load results: concentrate documented outcomes in the first two quarters of the engagement rather than saving value for year two, because on a 2.6-year clock year two is often already the handover-and-review window.

第二件要做的是把機構記憶文件化，這一段屬於我們的判斷而非報道內容。代理商真正的護城河，是它記得客戶的事比客戶自己記得多；一旦客戶端主管換人，這項優勢只有在寫下來的時候才發揮得出來。建議至少維護三份東西。一，決策記錄：每個重要決定的日期、當時的理由、否決了哪些選項、由誰拍板。新任 CMO 最常問的正是「為甚麼當初這樣做」，能當場答得出來的代理商，位置與要重新比稿的代理商完全不同。二，成效基線：每條產品線、每個市場的歷史表現區間與季節性，這樣新任主管提出的新目標可以被放回歷史脈絡討論，而非變成一場拍板定案的談判。三，一頁式交接簡報：常設給新任主管的版本，內容是合作範圍、已完成的事、正在跑的事、三個月內的決策點。The second thing to do is document institutional memory, and this paragraph is our judgment rather than reporting. An agency's real moat is remembering more about the client's business than the client does, and once the client-side lead changes, that advantage only pays out if it was written down. Maintain at least three artefacts. One, a decision log: the date of every significant decision, the reasoning at the time, the options rejected, and who signed off. What an incoming CMO asks most often is precisely why something was done that way, and an agency that can answer on the spot is in a very different position from one heading back into a pitch. Two, a performance baseline: historical result ranges and seasonality per product line and per market, so that new targets set by a new lead can be discussed against history rather than becoming a negotiation settled by assertion. Three, a standing one-page onboarding brief for new leads: scope of the engagement, what has been completed, what is running, and the decision points falling in the next three months.

最後是限定條件與過度解讀的風險。樣本全部來自美國，公司規模在 100 人以上，原料沒有任何香港或亞太的數字，因此不能把 2.6 年直接說成本地任期；合理的用法是把它當成方向性參考，再用自己客戶名單上過去三年的實際換人次數做本地校準，那份數字反而更有說服力。同時要留意平均值掩蓋了分歧：33% 在位超過五年，說明市場其實分成兩群人，一群很快離開，一群長期在位，所以不應該假設每位客戶主管都是短任期，逐個客戶判斷會準確得多。還有一點值得誠實面對：CFO 的 2.1 年比 CMO 更短，所以把這一則說成「行銷是最不穩定的職位」會與原料附的對照數字相衝。用這一則的最佳方式，是把它變成一次內部盤點 —— 每個主要客戶，現任對口做了多久、關係有幾個人承載、決策記錄有沒有寫下來。三條問題答不出來的客戶，就是本季度要補的位置。Finally, the qualifiers and the over-reading risks. The sample is entirely US-based at companies of 100-plus staff, and the source carries no Hong Kong or Asia-Pacific figures at all, so 2.6 years cannot be presented as local tenure. The sensible use is as directional reference, then calibrate locally by counting actual client-side changes across your own roster over the past three years — that number will carry more weight in the room anyway. Note too that the average conceals a split: 33% have held the post beyond five years, which says the market is really two populations, one leaving quickly and one staying long, so do not assume every client lead is short-tenured. Judging client by client will be considerably more accurate. One more point deserves honest treatment: the CFO's 2.1 years is shorter than the CMO's, so framing this item as proof that marketing is the least stable role in the C-suite contradicts the comparison figures the source itself supplies. The best use of this story is as an internal audit — for each major client, how long has the current counterpart been in post, how many people carry the relationship, and has the decision log actually been written. Any client where those three questions go unanswered is the gap to close this quarter.

本週可以動手的做法，是先在內部而非客戶帳號上做原型。挑一個低風險、不收集個人資料的場景，例如純資訊展示的門店指引、內部活動時間表、或一個只顯示內容的活動說明頁，用一句話生成，然後把整個過程記錄下來：輸入了甚麼描述、生成結果與預期差多少、要改幾輪才可看、預覽與發佈之間有沒有可插入的檢查點。這份記錄比任何評測都有用，因為它會直接告訴你哪些工序真的被省掉、哪些只是換了位置。同時建立一份內部清單，列明甚麼場景可以用生成式流程交付、甚麼場景一律走開發團隊。由於消息未交代推送範圍與帳號資格，第一步應該是確認你手上的帳號到底看不看得到這個功能，再決定要不要向客戶提起；在未確認之前就寫進提案，風險是承諾了一個客戶帳號根本用不到的流程。The hands-on step for this week is to prototype internally rather than on a client account. Pick a low-risk scenario that collects no personal data, such as a purely informational store guide, an internal event schedule, or a rules page that only displays content. Generate it from one sentence, then document the whole run: what description you typed, how far the output sat from expectation, how many revision rounds it took to become presentable, and whether any checkpoint can be inserted between preview and publish. That log is worth more than any external review, because it tells you which steps were genuinely removed and which merely moved. Alongside it, keep an internal list of which scenarios may ship through a generative flow and which always route to the dev team.

要說清楚的是一句話生成的小程序暫時不適合甚麼。凡涉及收集個人資料、會員登入、支付與交易、抽獎中獎名單與獎品發放、以及需要對外承諾穩定性的長期營運入口，都不應該由自助流程直接發佈，因為治理風險正是這項便利的鏡像：自助發佈會繞過原本由開發與法務把關的資料收集與送審環節。建議把發佈權限收在一個指定帳號，任何對外小程序在發佈前必須有人複核資料欄位、隱私說明與活動規則；同時在客戶合約與工作說明書中寫明，生成式流程產出的成果由誰負責維護與修補。這樣做的好處是你既吃到成本下降的空間，也不會把責任缺口留給自己。換句話說，這項功能改變的是誰能做出第一版，而非誰該為上線後的內容負責，把這兩件事分清楚，報價與風險控制就都站得住。It is worth stating plainly what a one-sentence-generated Mini Program is not ready for. Anything that collects personal data, handles member login, payments or transactions, manages prize draws and fulfilment, or serves as a long-lived public entry point with uptime expectations should not be self-published directly, because the governance risk is the mirror image of the convenience: self-serve publishing routes around the development and legal review that used to catch data-collection and approval problems. Keep publish rights on one designated account, require a named reviewer to check data fields, privacy wording and campaign rules before anything customer-facing goes live, and write into the contract or statement of work who owns maintenance and fixes for output produced through a generative flow. That way you capture the cost saving without inheriting an accountability gap.

Search Engine Journal 報道 Graphite 的《State of ChatGPT Ads》報告，數據來自 Similarweb 的面板。歷來點擊率最高的四個市場分別是巴西 1.91%、墨西哥 1.55%、日本 1.53% 與南韓 1.21%，而英語市場明顯低一截：加拿大 0.75%、英國 0.74%、美國 0.73%、澳洲 0.67%、紐西蘭 0.60%。報告另有美國曝光的類別分佈（3 月 30 日至 8 月 4 日）：B2B SaaS 佔 19.9%，涉及 1,210 個廣告主；零售與消費品牌佔 10.4%，涉及 1,510 個廣告主。日韓約為美國兩倍，是 AI 對話廣告版位在非英語市場更易轉化的第一個硬訊號。Search Engine Journal reports on Graphite's State of ChatGPT Ads study, which is built on Similarweb panel data. The four highest all-time click-through markets are Brazil at 1.91%, Mexico at 1.55%, Japan at 1.53% and South Korea at 1.21%, while English-language markets sit clearly lower: Canada 0.75%, the UK 0.74%, the US 0.73%, Australia 0.67% and New Zealand 0.60%. The report also breaks out US impressions by category for 30 March to 4 August, with B2B SaaS taking 19.9% across 1,210 advertisers and Retail and Consumer Brands 10.4% across 1,510 advertisers. Japan and South Korea outperforming the US by roughly two times is the first hard signal that AI-chat ad inventory converts better in non-English markets.

怎樣讀一個「超過 1%」的點擊率，決定你會不會做錯決定。先看它量的是甚麼：這是歷來（all-time）匯總數字，按市場合併，不是按廣告活動或版位拆分，所以任何一條個別素材的表現都無從得知。其次看量測條件：各市場的窗口由 5 週到 25 週不等，窗口長短本身就會改變平均值，短窗口更容易保留新版位的新鮮感效應。第三看披露程度：報告沒有公開曝光量，因此無法判斷這些百分比背後是幾千次還是幾百萬次曝光。第四看動機：數據背後的追蹤工具由 Similarweb 販售，這不代表數字有誤，只是提醒你它並非中立的行業基準。這則報道目前亦只有單一來源，建議自行覆核原始報告後才引用。How you read a headline CTR above 1% decides whether you make a bad call. Start with what it measures: these are all-time figures pooled per market, not split by campaign or placement, so nothing here tells you how any single creative performed. Next the measurement conditions: windows vary from 5 to 25 weeks by market, and window length alone shifts an average, with shorter windows more likely to preserve the novelty effect of new inventory. Then disclosure: no impression counts are published, so there is no way to tell whether a percentage rests on a few thousand impressions or a few million. Finally incentives: Similarweb sells the tracking tool behind the data, which does not make the numbers wrong, only means they are not a neutral industry benchmark. This is also a single-source report as we have it, so verify the original before citing it.

具體做法上，可以把它當成排測試的理由，不是設 KPI 的依據。第一步，先在自己的帳戶取基準：把同一產品線在現有搜尋與社交版位的點擊率、每次點擊成本與到站後的轉化率抄成一張對照表，沒有這張表，1.91% 沒有任何意義。第二步，設一個上限明確的測試預算，並預先寫下唯一的判斷指標——建議用到站後的合格線索或成交，而非點擊率本身，因為新版位的點擊率通常在初期偏高而後回落。第三步，訂一個最短觀察期與一條停損線，並在開始前就與客戶說明這是探索性投放，不進入年度成效承諾。第四步，把素材與落地頁的語言、貨幣與付款方式對齊目標市場，否則點擊會白費。Practically, treat it as a reason to schedule a test rather than a basis for setting a KPI. First, establish your own baseline: put the same product line's click-through rate, cost per click and post-click conversion rate from existing search and social placements into one comparison table, because without that table 1.91% means nothing. Second, set a hard-capped test budget and write down the single deciding metric in advance, ideally qualified leads or orders after landing rather than CTR itself, since new inventory usually runs hot early and settles later. Third, fix a minimum observation period and a stop-loss line, and agree with the client up front that this is exploratory spend outside annual performance commitments. Fourth, align creative and landing page language, currency and payment methods with the target market, or the clicks are wasted.

對香港廣告主而言，關鍵在於那四個市場是哪四個。日本與南韓上榜，意味這批版位在東亞消費者身上已經有可觀的互動率，做日韓出口、旅遊零售或跨境電商的客戶最值得先試；巴西與墨西哥的數字雖然更高，與本地客戶的關連通常較低。同時 OpenAI 在 9 月下旬向東南亞與台灣推開，令大中華周邊的版位仍處於未定價狀態，早期測試的相對成本優勢正在這個時間窗口裡。香港本身並不在上述任何一組數字之中，所以任何推算都只能當假設；較實際的安排是用日語、韓語或繁中素材各跑一組小額測試，用自己的數據建立本地基準，而不是把別人的市場平均值搬進媒體計劃書。此外，美國曝光的類別分佈顯示 B2B SaaS 與零售消費品牌合計已佔近三成，反過來說，這類客戶的競爭出現得最早，若你的客戶正屬其中，早試的意義比其他類別更大。For a Hong Kong advertiser the important part is which four markets these are. Japan and South Korea appearing on the list means the inventory already draws meaningful engagement from East Asian consumers, so clients selling into Japan or Korea, travel retail and cross-border commerce are the obvious first candidates, whereas Brazil and Mexico post higher rates with usually lower relevance to a local roster. At the same time OpenAI's late-September rollout across Southeast Asia and Taiwan leaves Greater China-adjacent inventory unpriced, and the relative cost advantage of an early test lives inside that window. Hong Kong itself appears in none of these figures, so any extrapolation is a hypothesis at best. The realistic plan is a small parallel test in Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese creative to build a local baseline from your own data, instead of importing someone else's market average into a media plan.
